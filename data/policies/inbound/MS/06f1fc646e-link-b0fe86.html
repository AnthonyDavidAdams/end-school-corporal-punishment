Statewide Accountability Performance Results

The Mississippi Statewide Accountability System is a single “A” through “F” school and district accountability system. Grades are based on student achievement, student growth, student participation in testing, and other academic measures.

Helpful Tips

Home Page

  • 1
    Search for schools and districts

    To search for a school or district, start typing the name of any school and district and make your selection from the list.

  • 2
    MSRC Featured Links

    This section of the site allows you to quickly access statewide summaries of data, including data displayed on a map and downloads. It also contains links to user guides and videos for navigating the site.

School and District Pages

  • 1
    Quick View

    The report card page for every school and district provides an overview of performance data. It also allows you to save or print a summary report.

  • 2
    Explore Data

    Click on a data point to see more details about the school or district performance in that area, including a comparison of different student groups. You can also see how the school or district compares to those around it in the map view.

Detail Pages

  • 1
    Comparisons

    When viewing a school, you will see how a school is performing in comparison to the district and the state.

  • 2
    Student Group Data

    See how different students groups compare to each other within a school or district.
