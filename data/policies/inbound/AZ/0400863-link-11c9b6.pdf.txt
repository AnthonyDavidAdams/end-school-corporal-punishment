Closed Campus Policy

Grades 7-12

Paideia Academies Inc. is a closed campus school. This policy is established to protect scholar
safety, preserve instructional time, support attendance expectations, and maintain a focused
academic environment for scholars in grades K-12.

Once a scholar arrives on campus, the scholar must remain on campus and in assigned
supervised areas until the scholar is officially dismissed, signed out through the front office,
released to an approved school activity, or otherwise authorized by the Principal or designee.
Scholars may not leave campus during the school day, including lunch, passing periods,
advisory, study periods, or before/after scheduled classes, without prior authorization from
school administration.

For purposes of this policy, “campus” includes the Paideia Elementary Campus and Paideia
Liberal Arts Academy school grounds, buildings, parking areas, school transportation, school-
sponsored activities, field trips, athletic events, clubs, internships, service learning, and any other
location where a scholar is participating in a Paideia-approved academic or extracurricular
activity. This policy is consistent with the current guidebook language that Paideia’s Code of
Conduct applies to school activities on and off campus, including bus travel, field trips, and
interactions before and after school.

South Mountain Community College Concurrent Enrollment Extension of
Campus

Paideia-approved Concurrent enrollment activities at South Mountain Community College are
considered an extension of the Paideia Liberal Arts Academy campus. Scholars participating in
Concurrent enrollment courses, placement testing, advising, orientation, library use, tutoring,
college visits, or any other Paideia-approved activity at South Mountain Community College
remain under the academic, behavioral, attendance, and safety expectations of Paideia Liberal
Arts Academy.

While participating in Concurrent enrollment activities at South Mountain Community College,
scholars must:

1. Attend only the approved class, activity, meeting, or supervised location assigned by
Paideia and/or South Mountain Community College.

2. Remain in authorized areas of the college campus.

3. Follow the directions of Paideia staff, South Mountain Community College personnel,
instructors, security staff, and other designated supervising adults.

4. Comply with the Paideia Code of Conduct, attendance expectations, technology
expectations, dress expectations, and all applicable South Mountain Community College
rules.

5. Leave the South Mountain Community College campus only through the transportation
or release plan approved by Paideia administration and the parent/guardian.
6. Refrain from inviting or meeting unauthorized indiviConcurrents during Paideia-
approved Concurrent enrollment time.

7. Refrain from leaving the assigned college area to visit restaurants, stores, parking lots,
nearby businesses, other college buildings, or other locations unless specifically
authorized by Paideia administration or the supervising adult.

Participation in Concurrent enrollment is a privilege. Scholars who violate the closed campus
policy during Concurrent enrollment activities may lose the privilege of participating in
Concurrent enrollment activities and may be subject to additional disciplinary consequences.

Leaving Campus Without Permission

A scholar may not leave the Paideia campus, South Mountain Community College Concurrent
enrollment activity area, or any Paideia-sponsored location without permission from school
administration or the supervising adult. Leaving without permission includes, but is not limited
to:

e Walking off campus.

e Leaving with another scholar.

e Leaving with an unauthorized adult.

e Going to a vehicle without permission.

e Leaving for lunch, snacks, drinks, or personal errands.

e Using a rideshare service, taxi, public transportation, or private ride without prior school
authorization.

e Leaving a South Mountain Community College class, building, or assigned location
without authorization.

e Failing to report to the assigned Paideia staff member or designated check-in/check-out
location.

Leaving campus or an assigned activity area without permission is a serious safety violation and
may result in parent/guardian notification, an attendance infraction, loss of privileges, removal
from an activity or Concurrent enrollment opportunity, a required parent meeting, demerit points,
suspension, or other disciplinary action as determined by the Principal or Dean of Students.

Parent/Guardian Release During the School Day

Scholars may only be released during the school day through the approved Paideia check-out
procedure. A parent/guardian or authorized emergency contact must check in at the front office,
present identification, and sign the scholar out. Scholars may not be released by phone call, text
message, email, or direct message alone.

For safety reasons, parents/guardians may not go directly to classrooms, activity areas, parking
lots, field trip locations, South Mountain Community College classrooms, or other off-campus
locations to remove a scholar without following the approved release procedure. The guidebook
already states that scholars must remain in school until dismissal, that early pick-ups count as
attendance infractions, and that scholars removed early must be signed out through the office.
Lunch and Food Delivery

Paideia Liberal Arts Academy does not operate as an open lunch campus. Scholars may not leave
campus for lunch or receive permission to leave campus to purchase food or drinks. Scholars
may not order or receive food through delivery services during the school day unless specifically
authorized by administration for a school-approved purpose.

Authorized Exceptions

The Principal or designee may authorize a scholar to leave campus or participate in an off-
campus activity for approved educational, medical, emergency, or family reasons. Authorized
exceptions may include:

e Parent/guardian check-out through the front office.

e School-sponsored field trips.

e Athletic events or extracurricular activities.

e Concurrent enrollment activities at South Mountain Community College.
e Approved internships, service learning, or capstone-related experiences.
e Medical appointments or emergencies.

e Other activities approved in advance by the Principal or designee.

All exceptions must include appropriate supervision, parent/guardian communication when
required, and a clear transportation and release plan.

Transportation Expectations

Scholars must follow the transportation plan approved by Paideia and the parent/guardian.
Scholars may not change transportation plans without approval. This includes transportation to
and from Paideia, South Mountain Community College, field trips, athletic events, internships,
and other school-sponsored activities.

When Paideia provides or arranges transportation, scholars must travel to and from the activity
using the approved transportation unless a parent/guardian release has been approved in writing
by administration. The existing guidebook already treats school transportation and field trips as
extensions of school expectations, with scholars under the authority of the approved vehicle
operator and supervising adults.

Emergency Situations

During an emergency, drill, lockdown, shelter-in-place, evacuation, or reunification process,
scholars must remain with the assigned staff member or supervising adult and follow all
directions. Parents/guardians should not attempt to remove scholars directly from campus, South
Mountain Community College, or another school-sponsored location unless directed by Paideia
administration or emergency officials.
Discipline and Loss of Privileges

Violations of the Closed Campus Policy may be addressed through Paideia’s Code of Conduct.
Consequences may include, but are not limited to:

e Parent/guardian notification.

e Attendance infraction.

e Restorative conference.

e Loss of off-campus privileges.

e Loss of Concurrent enrollment participation privileges.

e Removal from a field trip, activity, club, sport, or internship.
e Demerit points.

e Suspension.

e Referral to administration for further disciplinary action.

Arizona law provides that pupils must comply with school rules and submit to the authority of
teachers, administrators, and the governing board. Arizona law also prohibits interference with or
disruption of an educational institution.

Final Authority

The Principal or designee has final authority to interpret and enforce this policy in a manner that
protects scholar safety, preserves instructional time, and supports the mission of Paideia Liberal
Arts Academy.

Approval & Adoption:
This policy is subject to approval by the Paideia Academies Corporate Board of Directors.
Amendments or modifications require board authorization.
