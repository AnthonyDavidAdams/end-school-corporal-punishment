Eligibility Requirements

In order to enrolled, a child must be four years of age on September 1 and be a resident of Lee County, Georgia.

You must provide the following documents:

  • Certified Birth Certificate
  • Social Security Card
  • Driver’s License of the parent/guardian enrolling the student
  • Proof of Residency – A copy of the mortgage or lease agreement and a current power bill in the parent/legal guardian's name with physical address on it.
  • Ga. Certificate of Vision, Hearing, Dental, and Nutrition Screening (Form 3300)
  • Ga. Immunization Certificate (DHR Form 3231)

If applicable:

  • Custody portion of divorce decree
  • Letter of guardianship
