Strategic Waiver System

In accordance with the State of Georgia Article 4, O.C.G.A. § 20-2-84, it is the intent of the Lee County School District to operate as a Strategic Waiver school system through the development of the Strategic Waiver partnership contract between our school district and the State Board of Education (SBOE). The partnership contract will provide flexibility from Title 20 laws and State Board rules in return for increased academic accountability. The contract includes the flexibility waivers sought and proposed to the SBOE, the accountability targets required for each school, and the intended consequences for schools that do not reach their targets. The contract will begin on July 1, 2024 and will be in place for seven years with five years of accountability. The College and Career Readiness Performance Index (CCRPI) is the accountability tool used for determining progress. Each school's CCRPI target measurements will be sent to the Governor's Office of Education and Workforce Strategy on an annual basis. Consequences will be imposed during the seventh year if accountability goals are not met.
