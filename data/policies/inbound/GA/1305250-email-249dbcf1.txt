Board Policy JDA: Corporal Punishment
Original Adopted Date: 04/21/2015; Last Revised Date: 12/17/2019; Last
Reviewed Date: 12/17/2024

Any principal employed by the Union County Board of Education may
administer corporal punishment to any pupil or pupils under his or her care
and supervision. Any principal may designate the assistant principal or
any teacher employed by the board who is under the supervision of the
principal to administer corporal punishment. No other employee of the Board
or any other person, including the parents or guardian of such child or
children, may administer corporal punishment in the school. The following
requirements must be strictly adhered to in the administering of corporal
punishment:

 1. The corporal punishment shall not be excessive or unduly severe.
 2. Corporal punishment should be discouraged as a first line of
 punishment for misbehavior unless the pupil was informed beforehand that
 specific misbehavior could occasion its use.
 3. Corporal punishment must be administered in the presence of the
 principal, assistant principal, or the designee of the principal employed
 by the board of education, and the witness must be informed beforehand and
 in the presence of the pupil of the reason for the punishment.
 4. The principal, assistant principal or teacher who administered
 corporal punishment must provide the child's parents, upon request, a
 written explanation for the reasons for the punishment and the name of the
 principal or assistant principal, or designee of the principal who was
 present.
 5. Corporal punishment shall not be administered to a child whose
 parents or legal guardian have filed with the principal of the school a
 statement objecting to its use or a statement from a medical doctor
 licensed in Georgia stating that corporal punishment is detrimental to the
 child's mental or emotional stability.
 6. Corporal punishment shall be administered in private and not in the
 presence of other students.
 7. Corporal punishment shall be administered in the presence of the
 person administering such punishment and at least one witness, all of whom
 must be professionally certified employees working at this school.

 8. Corporal punishment shall not be administered to a student who
 refuses to accept corporal punishment. The parents or guardians of such
 child shall be consulted by the school principal before the principal
 takes further action.

 9. Where corporal punishment has been tried on frequent or several
 occasions and the conduct of the child has not improved, the principal
 shall explore the use of other forms of punishment and may refer the child
 in question to the board of education for other forms of discipline.
