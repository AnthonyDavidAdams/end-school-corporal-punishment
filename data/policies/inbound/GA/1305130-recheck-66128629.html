This handbook is published to acquaint you with the philosophy, policy, and rules of the Trion City Schools Board of Education and Trion High School for the school year.
 
1. Please note that policies and procedures listed in the handbook may be only PARTIAL policies and that other requirements may be added or changed. A more complete outline of policies may be found in the Board Policy Manual available in the principal’s office, library, central office or online at www.gsbaepolicy.org.
 
2. Please review the contents of this handbook, and if you have any questions, please contact the administration or any member of the faculty or staff.
 
3. After you read and understand the contents, please sign the acknowledgement on the enrollment data update form and return it to the office.
