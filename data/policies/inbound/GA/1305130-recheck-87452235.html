Enrollment Information

For the 2026–2027 school year, tuition for out-of-district students will be $200 per student, with a family maximum of $450.

Tuition must be paid in full before your child begins attending classes. Payments will open when student enrollment begins.

Be sure to check our website for enrollment dates and important updates.

Returning Students

Returning families can complete the enrollment form through your student’s PowerSchool Parent Portal account.

If you need assistance accessing your portal, please contact the school office — we’re happy to help!

Required Items

  • Trion City Residents
    • Own: (Required) City of Trion Water and Gas Bill and/or Proof of property ownership
    • Rent: (Required) Copy of the lease agreement signed by the property owner and lessee/applicant and/or Town of Trion issued water and gas bill received in your name at the property address.
  • Non-Residents: Tuition fees must be submitted to process the student application. If a payment plan is needed, please contact the Trion Middle School office.

New Students

If you are enrolling a new student, please complete the enrollment application below and return it to the TMS office with all required documents.

If you have any questions about the process or need assistance, our office is happy to help!

Required Items

  • TCS Enrollment Form
  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Social Security Card (copy)
  • School Certificate of Immunization on GA Form 3231
  • Vision, Hearing, and Dental Certificate on GA Form 3300
  • Parent/ Guardian State-Issued Picture ID (copy)
  • Custody Order (if applicable)
  • Current Academic Transcript
  • Current Attendance Records
  • Current Discipline Records
  • Proof of Residency (Trion City Residents only)
    • Own: (Required) City of Trion Water and Gas Bill and/or Proof of property ownership
    • Rent: (Required) Copy of the lease agreement signed by the property owner and lessee/applicant and/or Town of Trion issued water and gas bill received in your name at the property address.
