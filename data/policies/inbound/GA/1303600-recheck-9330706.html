Board of Education

Welcome to McIntosh County Board of Education

The McIntosh County Board of Education would like to provide each student and staff member with the tools necessary to enable students to maximize their educational growth and achieve academic excellence. Our goal is to offer our students a broad range of instructional methods and programs in order to accommodate all learning styles.  Our school system is relatively small in number with approximately 1400 total students in grades Pre-K through 12th grade. There are three schools in our district; Todd-Grant Elementary School serves Pre-K through 5th grade, McIntosh County Middle School serves 6th through 8th grade, and McIntosh County Academy is our high school and serves grades 9th through 12th. The Board of Education is proud of our teachers and staff members. They are dedicated to ensuring all of our students succeed and continually work to incorporate new technologies in the classroom. McIntosh County is a great community and we are proud of it!
