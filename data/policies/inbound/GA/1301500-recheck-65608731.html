Parents' Right to Know

Parents Right to Know

At the beginning of each year, school districts must notify parents of children attending Title I schools that they can request information regarding their children's teachers, including, at a minimum, information on 1) completion of state requirements for licensure and certification;2) emergency or other provisional status; 3) educational background; and 4) whether paraprofessionals are serving the child and, if so, the paraprofessionals' qualifications. Coweta County School System extends this notification request to all schools (Title I and Non Title I schools). If you have questions, please contact Mrs. Stefanie Easterwood at 770-254-2765.
