New Hires

Thank you for joining the Midland ISD family. We’re glad you’re here!

When your recommendation for hire is approved, you will receive an email about pre-employment requirements. This could include information about getting fingerprinted, obtaining official transcripts or certification information.

Once your pre-employment requirements are fulfilled, you will receive an email with an invitation to sign up for your new employee orientation. You’ll be scheduled to attend orientation once your information has been entered into the employee database.

New Employee Orientation

In the new employee orientation, you will learn about:

  • Online resources for employees
  • Board policy
  • Payroll and leave
  • Appraisal systems
  • Information technology
  • Retirement
  • Benefits
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Risk Management
  • Mandatory training

Feel free to review the resources above for more information.
