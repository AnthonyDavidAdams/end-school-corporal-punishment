Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Danville Community School Corporation accept transfer students? Is there a tuition cost for these students?

Yes, Danville Community School Corporation accepts transfer students at no charge. For more information see our Registration page.

Who do I contact with enrollment questions or to register my child?

Contact the building your child will be enrolling in. For contact information for each building see our Offices and Schools page.

What items are needed to register my child?

Items needed for registration are listed on our Registration page.

What are the school hours?

Office Hours:

  • High School 7:30 a.m. - 4 p.m.
  • Middle School 7:30 a.m. - 4 p.m.
  • South Elementary 7:15 a.m. - 4 p.m.
  • North Elementary 7:15 a.m. - 4 p.m.
  • DCHS Alternative Education Program (formerly Central Normal Campus) 7:30 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.

Student Hours:

  • High School 8:45 a.m. - 3:35 p.m.
  • Middle School 8:33 a.m. - 3:24 p.m.
  • South Elementary 7:40 a.m. - 2:25 p.m.
  • North Elementary 7:45 a.m. - 2:10 p.m.
  • DCHS Alternative Education Program (formerly Central Normal Campus) 7:30 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.

Where can I find school lunch information?

Click the Menus link at the top of any page. For additional information including applications for free or reduced price meals, go to our Food Services page.

How do I find out what bus my child rides?

For questions regarding your child's bus route or driver, please call the Transportation Department at 317-745-6875.  You can also visit our Transportation page to learn more about Stopfinder and how you can use it to track your bus route!

What is the process for volunteering at my child’s school?

We invite you to take an active role in the lives of Danville students as a volunteer. Join us in making a DIFFERENCE in the lives of Danville students and become a volunteer today. For information on volunteering at your child's school visit our Volunteer Opportunities page.

What kind of technology is available to my child at Danville Community Schools?

Danville Community School Corporation offers a wide variety of technology including a 1-1 program for grades 3-12. This means each student has their own personal device.

Where do I find information about the curriculum offered at Danville Community School Corporation?

Please visit our Curriculum and Instruction pages.

What kinds of clubs, sports, and extracurricular activities are available for my child participate in?

Danville offers a wide range of IHSAA approved sports and is competitive on the local and state level. Visit our Activities page for clubs and extracurricular information or our Athletics page at the appropriate building for more information on sports. You can also visit our Community page for local community organizations. 

Where can I find the district calendar with school start / end dates and holidays?

The Calendar link on any page will provide this information in addition to other activities and events.

Where can I find information about closings/delays? How will I be notified?

In the event of school cancellations, delays or early dismissals, we send alerts through our communications platform ParentSquare to parents and guardians. We will also share this information on our website and our Facebook and Twitter accounts @DCSCWarriors. In addition, we will report closings and delays to following television stations: WRTV Channel 6, WISH TV Channel 8, WTHR Channel 13, and WXIN Channel 59. For more urgent matters or time-sensitive matters, we may call home and cell numbers. When you verify your child’s demographic information each year, please make sure to provide accurate phone numbers to your child’s school.

To enhance communication between parents and guardians, the Danville Community School Corporation utilizes ParentSquare. You can learn more about ParentSquare here.

How many students attend Danville Community School Corporation?

Danville has a stable student population of approximately 2,600 students in grades KG through 12.

What grade did Danville Community School Corporation earn from the State Board of Education?

The Danville Community School Corporation is proud to be recognized as an “A” school district. Danville is also unique in that all of our buildings also received an “A” grade from the State of Indiana.

Does Danville offer an after school or before school program?

Yes, the Danville PACE program offers services from 6 a.m. - 6 p.m. For more information please see our PACE page or contact North Elementary or South Elementary.
