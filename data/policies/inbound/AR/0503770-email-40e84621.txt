Well I shredded the report that showed what the incidents were. I can't
recall what they were listed as but corporal punishment is always an option
and never a mandate. The students are given the choice of paddling vs
inschool suspension or paddling vs detention or paddling vs out of school
suspension (in certain cases only) example a student that might be sent
home for a day, the parent might request a paddling instead to keep the
student in class. All corporal punishment issues involve parent making the
ultimate decision as to administer or not. In case of the older students,
they will sometimes request the swats over the other option whether it be
detention, ISS, OSS. There are instances where the older students are not
allowed to opt out. Examples of some cases of bullying require time versus
getting a paddling and moving on. In those cases you need to make them
think about their actions and a paddling doesn't make them think about it
for long.
Hope this helps
