Student Handbook

Conduct and Policies

Building Respectful Communities

The Student Handbook is an essential resource designed to empower families with essential information on school policies and guidelines. It governs attendance, conduct, and discipline aligning with standards set by the Fort Smith Public Schools Board of Education. Discipline, attendance, and academic effort are fundamental to successful learning, and clear communication of expectations ensures a cohesive school environment.

Our commitment is to foster a safe, inclusive learning environment where every student can excel academically and socially. This handbook  ensures compliance with federal and state statutes while reflecting community values of positive learning. We encourage parents, students, and educators to collaborate in maintaining fair and consistent disciplinary practices.

Please take some time to review this handbook carefully, which outlines policies on attendance, student health, parental involvement, academic standards, extracurricular eligibility, and more. It serves as a foundation for promoting positive student conduct and ensuring a supportive educational experience for all.

Setting Standards, Inspiring Achievement

At Fort Smith Public Schools, our student handbook reflects our commitment to nurturing every child's academic, social, and personal growth. By outlining clear expectations and fostering a supportive learning environment, we aim to:

  • Empower students to excel academically.
  • Develop essential life skills.
  • Promote positive behavior among students.
  • Encourage active parental involvement.
  • Ensure a safe and inclusive school community.

Together, we strive to inspire a love for learning and prepare students for future success in an ever-changing world.
