Welcome to Dickson County Schools

Kindergarten Online Registration will begin April 21, 2025
PreK Online Registration will begin April 1, 2025

We would like to welcome you and your student to the Dickson County school system. Enrollment forms and information can be found using the links to the left or right. In the event you have questions or need more information, please feel free to use the contact information below.

Individual School Contact Information

For attendance, calendar, school policy questions send an email to: [email protected]

For questions regarding secondary education send an email to: [email protected]

For questions regarding specific student-related elementary education, such as schedules, attendance, supply information,
contact your child's school using the contact information provided on the school information page.

For questions regarding district-level elementary education, such as district selected curriculum, testing or assessment data,
send an email to: [email protected]

School Zoning Information or Transportation Department Ph: (615) 740-5970
Detailed guide for PreK and Kindergarten enrollment can be found here: Pre-K & Kindergarten Enrollment Playbook 
 
First time ever enrolling a student in Dickson County Schools? Skyward Account Creation 
 
Have you before or do you currently have a Dickson County School student? You may access your Family Access portal for a direct link to enroll your child or Dickson County Schools Skyward under Family/Student Access. 
 
If you do not have your login information for Family Access, you may click the Forgot your Login/Password? link, check your e-mail for a reset link sent yesterday, or you may contact your child's school staff.
 
The ability to complete an application is turned on for all guardians in Family Access so please submit only one application per child.
