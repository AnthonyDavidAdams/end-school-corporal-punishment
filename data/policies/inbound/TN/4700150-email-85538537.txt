It is the policy of the Millington Municipal Schools Board of Education
that corporal punishment shall not be used as a disciplinary measure in any
school.1 The director of schools shall be responsible for developing and
implementing in-service training programs for teachers and staff in the use
of alternative, positive measures of discipline.
