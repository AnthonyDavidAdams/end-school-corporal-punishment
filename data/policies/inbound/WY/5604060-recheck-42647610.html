Free & Reduced Meal Application

Instructions

The Free & Reduced Meal application can be filled out online through your Parent Infinite Campus account or a physical copy can be picked up at any school location or the District Office. Additionally, you can downloaded the application below.

Online Application

  1. Login to Infinite Campus. If you do not have a login or have forgotten your password, please contact a school secretary.
  2. Within Infinite Campus on the left menu, select More.
  3. On the resulting page, select Meal Benefits. There will then be a blue link to start the application process.

If you have any questions about how to define a household member, what counts as income, etc. Please see the “How-to” article below.

Infinite Campus Login

Physical Application

  1. Pick up the application at any school location (including the District Office) or download and print off via the buttons below.
  2. Use a pen to complete the form.
  3. Return the form to LCSD#2.
