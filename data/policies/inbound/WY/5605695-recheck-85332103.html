Welcome Back!

By Jessica Madden | Published August 26, 2026

Dear SCSD2 Families,

  • School Meals: Elementary school and John C. Schiffer students will receive free lunch through the Community Eligibility Provision (CEP) program again this year. For other students, households can fill out an application for free and reduced pricing eligibility based on household income via the parent portal.  If you require a paper copy, please contact your child’s school.
    • Free and Reduced Application Information. 
  • Transportation: For transportation information such as bus number, stop location, and times, please call 672-3105. Transportation information is also available on our website.
  • Registration: If you have not yet completed the annual update for your child(ren), you will find instructions on our registration web page.
  • Graduation Standards: 9-12 families can access information regarding Graduation Standards using this link.  
  • School Information:
    • For junior high and high schools, please view additional back to school information by following this link and selecting your school. 
    • School supply lists are available on the district website.
