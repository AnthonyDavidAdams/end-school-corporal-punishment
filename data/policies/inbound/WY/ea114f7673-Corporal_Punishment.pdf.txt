CORPORAL PUNISHMENT 4080

In elementary schools which employ corporal punishment a record
shall be kept of incidents which are resolved by corporal punishment.
Such records will show the name, sex, and ethnic group of the student
and be accessible to the Central Administration for the purpose of
monitoring such cases as well as furnishing documentation in any hearing
pertaining to the disciplining of the child.

All forms of corporal punishment shall be banned in the middle
school and high school of this District. No paddle or other means of
inflicting such punishment shall be retained on school premises by any
middle school or high school teacher or administrator. Circumvention of
the policy will not be allowed for any reason. The penalty for vio-
-lation may be any penalty found by the Board to be appropriate to the
offense including discharge.

Adopted April 9, 1985
