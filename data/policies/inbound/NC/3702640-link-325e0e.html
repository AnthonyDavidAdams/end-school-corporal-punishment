Welcome

Welcome to our Community by Diligent public site, where you can find information about upcoming and recent meetings and participate in discussions about our community.


Upcoming meetings

A list of upcoming meetings
  • You can access the meeting agenda and supporting materials
  • If there is a live stream of the meeting, you can view a broadcast of the meeting in progress

Recent meetings

A list of the most recent meetings
  • You can view the meeting agenda and supporting materials
  • Review the meeting minutes to see what was discussed and decided in the meeting
  • If the meeting is recorded, you can access the recording

Calendar

An easy to use calendar view of meetings
  • You can discover upcoming meetings and see which topics will be covered in the published agendas
  • Scan previous meetings and review their agendas, supporting documents, and minutes
  • See an overview of all meetings or focus on a specific type of meeting

Meetings

  • You can view upcoming meetings, sorted by type
  • Review the members in that meeting group

Search

  • You can search for topics of interest in upcoming and previous meetings

Subscribe

  • Sign up for email notifications about a specific type of meeting
