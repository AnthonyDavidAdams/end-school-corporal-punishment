Florida Statutes and School Board Policies

Per Florida Statue 1006.283, Collier County Public School's Instructional Materials Program adheres to a process that includes review, recommendation, adoption, and purchase of instructional materials. All parents and guardians have the ability to access and review their children’s instructional materials and are encouraged to access at any time.

Florida Statute 1006.28: Duties of district school board, district school superintendent; and school principal regarding K-12 instructional materials.

Instructional materials are selected in compliance with Florida Statute 1006.31 and Board Policy 2520. Parents interested in reviewing instructional materials or reviewing the Instructional Materials Work Group Calendar/Meetings and related documentation may contact (239) 377-0103 for more information.

In compliance with HB 1467, all elementary schools must publish on their website, in a searchable format, a list of all materials maintained in the school media center or requested as part of a school or grade-level reading list. Please visit your child's elementary school's webpage to access this information.
