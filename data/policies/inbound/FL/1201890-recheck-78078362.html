  • Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports

    The Union County School District complies with Florida law as it relates to positive behavior interventions and supports. The district currently utilizes the Florida Positive Behavioral Interventions and Support Project (Florida PBIS) as well as using Crisis Prevention Institute (CPI) and Totem PD (ASPEN) as a means of training our teachers and staff on positive behavior interventions and supports. CPI and ASPEN both focus on de-escalation. For more information regarding board-adopted policies, please go to the School Board tab and click on District Policies.
