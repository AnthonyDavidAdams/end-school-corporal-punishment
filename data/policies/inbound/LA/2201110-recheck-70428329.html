Support Services

"To educate all students to be productive

citizens in an ever-changing world."


Phone: (318) 283-1600

Fax: (318) 281-4910


Vision: The vision of the Morehouse Parish Schools Student Services Department is to work collaboratively with teachers, service providers, school administration, students, and parents to promote student achievement. The student support services department will make student-centered decisions to support individual and collective needs, provide training, education, and promote high expectations in order to empower students with disabilities through self-advocacy for success in the paths they choose and pursue to be productive citizens in an ever-changing world. 


GOALS: Education, Advocacy, Empowerment, Advancement
