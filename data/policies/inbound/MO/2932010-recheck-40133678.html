By The Numbers

400+ Students

Students

Willard students attend school in nine buildings—five elementary schools, two intermediate schools, one middle school, and one high school. 

700+

Staff Members

With over 700 dedicated staff members, we provide comprehensive support and exceptional educational experiences for our students.

18:1

Student : Teacher Ratio

Our 18:1 student-teacher district ratio surpasses the DESE standard, ensuring personalized attention and superior educational outcomes.

94.9%

Attendance Rate

Our impressive 94.9% district attendance rate highlights our community's commitment to education, showing a significant increase post-COVID.
