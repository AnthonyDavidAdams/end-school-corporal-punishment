Enrollment Information

Welcome to Willard—we're glad you're here!

Our district proudly serves roughly 4,200 students and 700 staff members, and we are committed to providing a high-quality education and experience to each and every one of them. We are so excited to have you join our community. Calling or visiting the District Office is the initial step toward enrollment in the Willard Public School District. Below you will find information, links, and directions on how to successfully enroll your student in Willard Public Schools. 

Enrollment FAQs

  • What documents do I need to enroll my student?
  • Who should I contact to answer my questions about enrolling my child?
  • How do I know which school my student will attend?
  • Where can I find numbers for my students' school?

What documents do I need to enroll my student?

The following documents are required for enrollment and may be submitted electronically. If you are not able to upload the documents electronically, please call the Willard District Office at 417-742-2584 to schedule a time to come in. 

  • Proof of Residency - must provide one of the following documents that are dated within the last 60 days, lists the SERVICE address, company's name, and the parent/guardian's name. If you need to complete a multifamily form, please contact the District Office. 
    • Utility Bill/Agreement
    • Lease Agreement/Rental Receipt
    • Mortgage Statement
    • Legal Property Description-Deed
    • Real Estate Property Tax Receipt
  • Birth Certificate (or copy)
  • Social Security Card (or copy)
  • Up-to-date Immunization Record

Who should I contact to answer my questions about enrolling my child?

For new and returning students attending Willard Public Schools, please call the District Office at 417-742-2584 or email enroll@wilIardschools.net with any questions regarding the enrollment process. You will then be directed on next steps. 

How do I know which school my student will attend?

Call the District Office at 417-742-2584 or use our interactive boundary map here. 

Where can I find numbers for my students' school?

See the District Directory for phone numbers.

Willard School District Health Information Form

Complete the Willard School District Health Information Form. This health form must be submitted each school year. Please note that there is no way to submit it directly online. You must download and complete the form and email/fax/mail it to your students' school nurse or drop off a hard copy. 

Transportation Request Form

To guarantee your student is assigned to a bus route and has transportation in place for the first day of school, all bus rider requests must be submitted by July 17, 2026.

ALL riders, including past and current, as well as those who have moved recently, must complete the Transportation Request form. 

If your address as changed, please contact the District Office with proof of residence at 417-742-2584. 

Free & Reduced Lunch Application

The Willard Public School District operates a meal assistance program, which complies with federal guidelines. It's easy (and fast!) to apply for meal assistance, free, or reduced meal price meals for our breakfast and lunch programs. Willard Public schools kindly asks every family to complete a Free & Reduced Meal Application. 

Information provided on the application is confidential and will be used only for the purpose of determining eligibility. If possible, please fill out the form online through the LINQ Connect Portal. Click here for a hard copy. The district will notify you within 10 school days, either by email or letter, if you qualify for free or reduced meals. If you do not receive a notice after 10 days, please contact Willard Nutrition Services Department at 417-742-2584.

2026-2027 Missouri School Immunization Requirements

Access the list of required school immunization requirements here. 

Missouri Law requires children enrolled in Kindergarten through 12th grade to be immunized against certain preventable diseases. Updated immunization records are required for students entering kindergarten, 8th grade, and 12th grade. All students must present documentation of an up-to-date immunization status, including month, day, and year of each immunization before attending school. For additional information or questions, please contact Cindy Henslee, RN, Director of Health Services at 417-742-3524.
