Thank you for your support

We are thrilled to announce that thanks to your support, the bond issue for school improvements has passed!  Your votes will help us move forward with modernizing and enhancing our district, creating a safer, more dynamic learning environment for our students. Here are the key areas that will benefit from this bond:

 Safety & Security

  • Secure entrances at both the High School & Primary School

  • Fire alarm upgrades at the Middle School

  • Storm shutters in the Middle School cafeteria

District wide Improvements

  • Roof and HVAC replacements

  • ADA upgrades in buildings and playgrounds

  • Addressing deferred maintenance across the district

Career & Tech Ed

  • Greenhouse renovation for the Agriculture program

  • Culinary Arts upgrades at both the High School and Middle School

Activities

  • New locker rooms, restrooms, weight room, and storage by Eklund Stadium

  • Expanded seating, new press box, and improved lighting at the stadium

Grade Transitions & Building Use

  • Middle School will be renovated for grades 6–8

  • Intermediate School reconfigured for grades 3–5

  • Elementary School repurposed for future uses such as Daycare, Central Office, or Alternative School

These improvements will ensure that our schools are better equipped for the future, enhancing educational opportunities and creating a more secure and modern environment for students, staff, and the entire community.

We couldn't have done this without your continued support. Thank you for helping us make these much-needed changes possible!  While the election results are unofficial at this time, we are excited about the possibilities ahead and look forward to the positive changes this will bring.
