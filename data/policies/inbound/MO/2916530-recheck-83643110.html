    October ROARing Tigers Keytesville R-III Elementary has a program called ROARing Tigers. Each month, students can earn a certificate for being a ROARing Tiger by displaying the characteristics in the ROARing Tiger acronym, which stands for: Respect, Optimism, Accountability, and Responsibility. In addition to displaying these characteristics, students must not have any discipline issues or office referrals, and no late work. At the end of each month, students who earn the reward are presented a certificate. Congratulations to all of our students! ...

    Keytesville R-III School District

    1 month ago

    Our 6th graders have spent the past week exploring the lives of the earliest humans and learning how they lived, created art, and developed tools for survival!Students stepped back in time with two hands-on activities that brought history to life. First, they learned about prehistoric cave paintings, including the natural materials early humans used to create paint and the images they painted on cave walls. To experience this for themselves, Mrs. Meyer turned off the classroom lights and gave each student a piece of crumpled brown paper to mimic the texture of a cave wall, along with red, black, and white paint similar to the colors used by early humans. While only having a small flashlight to represent the light from a fire, students created their own prehistoric masterpieces!Next, students learned about flint and how early humans shaped stone into tools and arrowheads. They put their knowledge to the test by carving their own arrowheads out of bars of soap using only a toothpick and a plastic butter knife as their tools. Students quickly discovered that creating tools by hand with limited resources required patience, time, and a lot of hard work! Some of them didn't quite finish! These activities gave students a fun, hands-on way to experience the creativity, resourcefulness, and challenges of life for the earliest humans! ...
