Our policy is MCE Policy 2670 - Corporal Punishment: Prohibited

No person employed by or volunteering for the School District shall
administer or cause to be administered corporal punishment upon a student
attending District schools.

A staff member may, however, use reasonable restraint against a student
without advance notice to the principal, if it is essential for
self-defense, the preservation of order, or for the protection of other
persons or the property of the School District.
