WELCOME TO BUILDING BLOCKS PRESCHOOL


We are so excited that you have chosen to enroll your child in our Fox C-6 School District’s Building Blocks Preschool. MISSION STATEMENT
Our Building Blocks’ mission is to provide a solid foundation for your child’s kindergarten readiness.
Explore, Get Set, and Go!
CURRICULUM
The Building Blocks Program utilizes the HighScope Curriculum as the foundation. HighScope is a DESE (Department of Elementary and Secondary Education) approved early childhood curriculum. High Scope students will:
* Become independent, responsible, and confident-ready for success in school.
* Learn to plan many of their own activities, carry them out, and talk with other children and their teacher about what they have done.
* Learn through active involvement with people, materials, events, and ideas.
* Gain knowledge and skills in important concept areas, such as creative representation, language and literacy, initiative and social relations, movement, music, classification, number, space, and time.
In addition to the HighScope Curriculum, our Building Blocks educators use the Fundations and Heggerty Pre-K curriculum.  Fundations is used to teach letter-sound associations and letter formation. The Heggerty curriculum introduces students to pre-reading skills and phonemic awareness skills through quick, engaging whole group lessons. The focus of these lessons is on exposure, not mastery. 
OUR PHILOSOPHY OF LEARNING
In a caring and positive elementary school atmosphere, we create a warm and happy place for preschoolers to learn. As we bridge the gap from home to school, we guide children to a continued good self-image while building social skills in a school setting. Sensory, motor, perceptual, and language skills are introduced through materials and activities which are both child-centered and teacher-directed. Work is planned, which emphasizes the process, rather than the product, fostering a sense of accomplishment and pride. The focus of learning is on readiness skills needed in kindergarten. Based on the theory that children learn through play, classroom routines encourage active involvement, meaningful experimentation, and reinforcement through repetition. Schedules are designed to balance structures and free choice, as well as active and quiet times. Recognizing that children grow in predictable stages, we treat each child as an individual, working from the level each child has attained and moving forward one step at a time. We teach a love of learning by allowing children to feel success without pressure.
OUR STAFF
Our Building Blocks lead teachers meet a minimum requirement of an Associate's Degree or an equivalent DESE approved experience; however, many have a Bachelor Degree in Education or a related field.  All staff members are certified in CPR and First Aid, background screened and fingerprinted, mandated to complete yearly clock hours of training, and receive professional development throughout the school year.
MIXED-AGE GROUPING
Each Building Blocks classroom uses mixed-age groupings.  This means there will likely be 3 year olds and 4 year olds in the same classroom.  Younger children benefit from the modeling of older children and older children can strengthen their skills and increase their confidence by helping younger children. 
PARENT INVOLVEMENT
We understand that the education of children is a partnership and we invite you to share your thoughts with us at any time.  We will make every effort to keep you informed about your child's growth at school.  In an effort to keep you informed of your child's progress, we will have two conferences a year to review your child's portfolio and assessments of your child's growth.  Please sign up for a conference time in November and April with your child's teacher.
In addition to conferences, you will have the ability to stay connected and communicate regularly with your child’s teaching staff. Our teachers will use classroom apps, such as SeeSaw, that allow them to post real time updates about what the children are doing in the classroom, pictures of the children having fun, and reminders about upcoming activities and school events. Our teachers will use email to correspond regularly, child folders that go home each day with your child, and of course phone calls.
ENTRANCE REQUIREMENTS
Building Blocks Preschool is a tuition-based preschool for Fox C-6 resident children that are between the ages of 3-5 years.  Our classrooms are located in various elementary schools throughout the district.  Entrance requirements include:
* Must be a Fox C-6 School District resident or a district employee.
* Must be 3 years of age on or before February 28, 2026.
* Must not turn 5 years of age on or before July 31, 2026. 
* Must be completely toilet trained & completely independent in the restroom. Building Blocks students will be using restrooms throughout the elementary buildings shared by other students in various grade levels. At first, these restrooms can be intimidating to the little ones, so we encourage parents to have their children using regular bathroom facilities (toilets and sinks) prior to starting the program.
* Parents must understand that unlike a “typical” preschool, their children will be in an elementary school building, in a regular classroom, following a school routine.

CHILD GUIDANCE POLICY/BEHAVIOR MANAGEMENT
The staff will carefully use conflict that arises in the classroom to help children learn about appropriate ways to vent anger and frustration and to solve everyday problems. The focus is on positive guidance to help children begin to learn about the effects of their actions on others and to find socially acceptable behaviors. The philosophy is based on respect for the child's self-esteem, setting reasonable limits and consequences, and encouraging increased self-discipline, while also promoting respect for each other. To provide a safe, positive learning environment, the staff will assist each child in becoming familiar with classroom rules and expectations. Staff will implement visual, tangible behavior chart systems, reward systems, and encouragement.
We believe in setting limits for children as they grow to let them know that they can count on the adults in their life to make them feel safe and secure about their environment. If your child's teacher has concerns that the child guidance implementations are not successful, a conference with the parent may be requested by the teacher. The purpose of this conference is to seek suggestions from the parent on how they may handle certain behaviors at home, to ensure that ongoing communication between teacher and parent is occurring, and to gain additional resources to help correct behaviors in the classroom. Such behavior concerns are, but not limited to, biting, kicking, hitting, punching, running away from the group, inconsolable temper-tantrums, and potty accidents.
Additionally, if an ongoing behavior concern does not seem to be improving after all options have been implemented, the teacher and director of the program may request a parent meeting to discuss whether Building Blocks is the right environment for the child. If a child is having a difficult day and is not complying with rules and expectations after additional redirecting, and all forms of guidance have not been successful, the parent may be called to have their child picked up early. If a pattern of parent pick up persists, and an ongoing behavior concern routinely disrupts the classroom environment, and the ability to implement lessons is difficult, then Building Blocks reserves the right to indefinitely suspend the child's enrollment in the program.

HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICIES
The Building Blocks staff makes every effort to ensure the safety and well-being of all children. We hope your child will be well and in attendance every day, however, illnesses are common with young children and if any sign of illness is present, please keep him/her home. The following symptoms could indicate a contagious disease. 
* Fever/pain                            
* Vomiting/Diarrhea
* Skin eruption/rash 
* Extreme fatigue
* Swelling/redness of throat 
* Reddened, weeping eyes
* Constant sneezing/productive cough 
Parents are requested to keep their child home if there is a presence of any of the above symptoms. The child will be admitted to class when he/she has been free of any and all of these symptoms and has not required fever/pain medication for a minimum of 24 hours. Should these symptoms develop while at school, the child will be cared for until parents can be notified to pick him/her up.  We ask that someone try to arrive within 30 minutes to pick-up the ill child. If a symptom persists, such as a rash/cough or if a communicable disease is confirmed such as measles, or chicken pox, admittance may require a statement from the child's physician declaring the condition non-contagious.
If your child has been diagnosed with strep, flu, or COVID,  please notify the teacher and the school nurse at your earliest convenience.
Children with head lice cannot attend school. Our policy states that children must be “nit-free” before returning to school to keep from spreading lice. The school nurse must check your child before he/she returns to the preschool program. 
POTTY TRAINING/ACCIDENT POLICY
The prerequisite of your child being completely toilet-trained and bathroom independent, is necessary because, unlike most nursery schools or daycares, our program operates in the elementary school buildings throughout the District. Our preschool children are using large restrooms, with multiple stalls of regular size toilets, and urinals. These restrooms are shared with other students and grade levels throughout the building. As a result of the overall privacy of other students, the Building Blocks teacher is limited in assisting a Building Blocks student who is using the restroom.  Toilet training is equally important because each classroom has 20 students, and frequent potty accidents pull an educator away from the other students, which creates an impact on the overall learning environment.  
Building Blocks does understand that a preschooler may have an infrequent accident. However, if a child has multiple accidents, Building Blocks will assume that potty training has not been achieved and will reserve the right to terminate or suspend service. If a bowel movement accident occurs, according to district policy, the child will be given the opportunity to independently clean themselves, and change into clean clothes. However, if this is not effective, we will require that a parent or an emergency contact pick the child up immediately. If the child does not have diarrhea, the child may return to Building Blocks when the child has been cleaned.  If the child does have diarrhea, the child must remain at home until he/she has been symptom free for 24 hours. Children who have urine accidents will have the opportunity to clean themselves and change clothes if a change of clothes has been provided. Regardless of what kind of accident, if there are no additional clean clothes available, the child will be required to be picked up.  Children may not always do a good job of cleaning themselves after a bowel movement. Building Blocks staff will show compassion, and help to a degree if possible, however, they are unable to completely clean or help children wipe.  If you are concerned about your child's cleanliness, we encourage you to provide wipes that we will have your child use when they use the restroom.
ILLNESS AND INJURY PROCEDURES
If a child becomes ill after arrival, a parent will be notified and expected to make arrangements to pick up the sick child as soon as possible, not to exceed one hour. If the school nurse is on duty, your child may remain with her until you are able to pick up; if the nurse is not on duty your child will be made as comfortable as possible in the classroom while he or she awaits your arrival.
Minor injuries, such as scratches, scrapes, insect bites, etc., are treated by the preschool staff or the school nurse.   Plenty of TLC is always given!
Any serious injury or head injury will be reported to the parents immediately so that appropriate action may be taken.
If a major injury occurs, the staff will immediately call for professional help (school nurse and/or 911). Parents, designated emergency contacts, or the child's doctor will be notified immediately.
If an emergency is such that immediate transportation to the hospital is necessary, a district staff member from Fox C-6 will accompany the child.
