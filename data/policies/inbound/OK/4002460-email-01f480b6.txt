To answer your questions: corporal punishment is typically reserved for
minor types of infractions or repeated classroom misconduct. Crucially, it
is only administered with the full cooperation and consent of the parent or
guardian—in fact, it is often requested by parents as an alternative to
detention or in-school suspension (ISS). As for frequency, our district
does not use it widely. It is rarely used at the high school level, and
only occasionally comes up at the middle and elementary school levels.

Hope this helps complete what you need! Please let me know if you have any
further questions.
