Experience:  

 

 I am a Pre-K teacher,  I have worked with littles before when I lived in Kansas. I look forward to getting to know them and teaching young minds this year. I love teaching and really enjoy working with students. I am married and have 4 children and 6 grandchildren and one more. My passion has and always has been to help people.  I wonder if our path of growing ever really stops? I have had many different pathways during my life. All of my careers in my life helped shape the person that I am today. I worked with elementary-age students at FCC church in the livewire program. I was the Drama Director at FCC church. Acting and teaching students through drama and music is a passion of mine. I can play several instruments and enjoy teaching the students about music. Science and Social studies are my two favorite subjects. It gives me great joy to teach your students about how things in our world work and about the history of our world, nation, and state. 

      I have chosen many careers during my life. Each career helped mold me into the teacher that I am now. I try to use all of my experience to encourage the students to reach forward with goals. I am listing some of the career paths that I have engaged in daycare provider, Cook, Waitress, Convenience Store Clerk, Pizza making queen, President of the Chetopa First Responders,  Paramedic Supervisor, Emergency Medical Service Training Tech. emergency room technician, Mobile intensive care technician, firefighter,  licensed cosmetologist, shade tree mechanic. Bus driver, and Teacher


      I love being a member of the Turkey Ford Family! I am so excited to be your student's teacher and I look forward to working with them. I am always available for questions or concerns. My school email is listed. But you can also reach me via, Class Tag or the Pre-k Website listed below.

Biography: 

 

I am a Christian and an active member at Mountain Movers Church located on 240 road ( Turkey Ford Area) Oklahoma.  I am a wife, my husband's name is Nicholas and he is a police officer. I have 5 children 4 living and 1 in Heaven. I have 6 grandchildren. 

I worked as a Paramedic in Oklahoma, Kansas, and Missouri for many years and as a Mobile Intensive Care Technician in Kansas for 15 years. I ran an ambulance volunteer program known as the Chetopa first responders for many years. I love helping others.

My true passion is teaching and I am so blessed to be able to teach your students.  I believe all students can and will learn in a good environment. Our classroom is a family atmosphere. I look forward to working with you and your families.
