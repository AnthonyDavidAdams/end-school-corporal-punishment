CORPORAL PUNISHMENT
(REGULATIONS)

The determination as to whether to administer corporal punishment will be made by the principal or teacher.
In each specific incidence, corporal punishment must be administered by the principal or teacher. Corporal
punishment administered by the teacher must be in the presence of the principal or another teacher. The prin-
cipal may administer the corporal punishment after conferring with the teacher. If the principal administers
the punishment, it is recommended that the teacher who referred the student be one of the witnesses.

Before administering corporal punishment, the administrator must explain the nature of the offense to the
student in the presence of the witness.

All cases of corporal punishment will be recorded in writing with a copy mailed to the parent(s) or legal
guardian. The report must include the signature of the witness.

Corporal punishment shall not violate the law.

A maximum of two swats will be given for each offensive incident.
