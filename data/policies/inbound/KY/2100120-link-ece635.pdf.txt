be held on May 22nd and will be conducted in the front office.

SBDM council members must participate in the annual SBDM training. This year it is held on
July 10th. The training for experienced members is from 12-3 and for new members is from
42-6.

The next SBDM meeting will be held on June 5, 2019 at 3:30 pm.
