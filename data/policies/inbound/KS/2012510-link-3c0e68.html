    Welcome to USD 262!

    USD 262, serving Valley Center, Park City, Kechi and Wichita, is committed to excellence in education, innovative instruction, outstanding programs and student success. Our dedicated staff and supportive communities work together to provide students with opportunities to learn, grow and prepare for their futures.
