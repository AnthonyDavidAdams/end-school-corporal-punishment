The Board of Education convenes on the second Monday of each month, unless an alternative date is established by board action. Individuals wishing to address the board are encouraged to contact the Board Clerk in advance of the upcoming meeting. Board members serve four-year terms and are elected to represent distinct geographical areas within the district.
