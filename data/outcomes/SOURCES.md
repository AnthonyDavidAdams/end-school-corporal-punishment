# Outcome data for the corporal punishment correlation study

Candidates, all public, all district-level, all joinable on the NCES LEA id:

- SEDA (Stanford Education Data Archive): district mean test scores on a common national scale,
  grades 3-8, math and reading, 2009-2019 (v5.0) with a 2023 release covering the pandemic years.
  The only cross-state comparable achievement measure. https://edopportunity.org/get-the-data/
- EDFacts / ED Data Express: Adjusted Cohort Graduation Rate by district, most recent 2022-23.
- CRDC 2023-24 LEA file: enrollment, corporal punishment counts, suspensions, expulsions, chronic
  absenteeism, referrals to law enforcement, all by district. https://civilrightsdata.ed.gov/data
- NCES CCD: enrollment, free/reduced lunch share, locale (rural/town/suburb/city) for controls.
