# Former students, 18 to 35

**Where they see images:** Instagram, TikTok, Reddit (r/teachers, r/mississippi, r/texas), group chats, X. They are the audience that carries a piece from a feed into a family text thread with "did you know this is still legal."

**Visual language they trust:** the screenshot and the receipt. A plain text card, a document with a highlight, a map with one state circled, a Notes-app confession, a tweet-style card, a paddle photographed like a product listing. Deadpan beats outrage; the fact does the work. High contrast, few words, mobile-first.

**What reads as an outsider:** polished campaign design, a logo in the corner larger than the fact, hashtags, inspirational fonts, anything a brand would post.

**Copy voice:** deadpan, first person, specific. "I was paddled in 4th grade in [town]. It is still legal there. Here is the count." The ask is small and native: send it to the group chat; sign the opt-out for your own kid; ask your mom whether she knew.

**Formats:** 1080x1350 and 1080x1920 story; plain-text card; a map crop.

**Concepts that fit:**
- Receipt-style card: "CORPORAL PUNISHMENT, US PUBLIC SCHOOLS, 2021-22. STUDENTS: 24,534. STATES: 15. SOURCE: US DEPT. OF EDUCATION."
- Map with the 15 states in red, caption "still legal in 2026."
- "It's a wooden board. It's in the principal's office. It's 2026." over a plain object photo.
- The 36-of-38 OECD line as a text card.
