# Public records request for district corporal punishment records

Every state has a public records law. This request asks for the records that show how often a district uses corporal punishment and on whom, without any student's identity. Send it to the district's records custodian (usually the superintendent's office); the district website often names the officer. Cite your state's statute in the first line: `data/states/<XX>.yaml` lists it under `records_law` where known.

```
[DATE]

[DISTRICT NAME]
Attn: Public Records Officer
[ADDRESS]

Re: Public records request under [STATE PUBLIC RECORDS STATUTE]

Dear Records Officer:

Under [STATUTE], I request copies of the following records for the [YEAR] and [YEAR] school years:

1. The district's current board policy and any administrative regulation or handbook section governing corporal punishment, including any parental consent or refusal form.
2. Aggregate counts, by school, of incidents of corporal punishment administered, and of students who received corporal punishment, with any breakdown the district maintains by grade, sex, race, and disability status. I am not requesting any personally identifiable student information; please redact as required by FERPA rather than withhold.
3. Any log, register, or report form the district requires to be completed when corporal punishment is administered, in blank, and the count of such forms completed per school.
4. Any complaint, claim, or notice of claim submitted to the district regarding corporal punishment, with personal information redacted.
5. The district's submission to the US Department of Education Civil Rights Data Collection for the most recent collection, limited to the corporal punishment items.

I request electronic copies. If any portion is withheld, please cite the specific exemption and release the remainder. If fees will exceed $[AMOUNT], please contact me before proceeding. Please acknowledge receipt within the time required by [STATUTE].

Sincerely,
[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[PHONE]
```
