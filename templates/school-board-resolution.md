Placeholders are in [BRACKETS]. Facts cited in this template are maintained in `facts/claims/`; check `facts/README.md` for the current figures before sending.

```
RESOLUTION OF THE [DISTRICT NAME] BOARD OF EDUCATION
PROHIBITING CORPORAL PUNISHMENT

Resolution No. [NUMBER]

WHEREAS, research consistently demonstrates that corporal punishment is ineffective as a discipline strategy and may cause physical and psychological harm to students; and

WHEREAS, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Psychological Association, the National Education Association, and other professional organizations oppose the use of corporal punishment in schools; and

WHEREAS, corporal punishment disproportionately affects students of color and students with disabilities; and

WHEREAS, effective alternative discipline strategies exist that promote positive behavior without physical punishment; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education is committed to providing a safe, supportive learning environment for all students;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the [DISTRICT NAME] Board of Education hereby:

1. Prohibits the use of corporal punishment by any employee, volunteer, or contractor in any school or program operated by the District;

2. Directs the Superintendent to develop policies and procedures implementing this resolution within 60 days;

3. Directs the Superintendent to provide training on alternative discipline strategies to all staff;

4. Commits to reviewing discipline data annually to ensure equitable treatment of all students.

ADOPTED this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR].

_______________________________
Board President
```
