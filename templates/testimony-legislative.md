# Written and oral testimony for a legislative committee

Committees read written testimony and hear two to five minutes of oral testimony. Written testimony should be one to two pages, cite sources, and be submitted through the committee's portal before the deadline. Oral testimony should be the first paragraph plus the ask.

```
Testimony of [NAME], [TITLE/AFFILIATION]
[COMMITTEE], [CHAMBER], [STATE] Legislature
In support of [BILL NUMBER], [DATE]

Chair [NAME] and members of the committee:

[STATE] is one of [NUMBER] states in which a public school employee may legally strike a student as punishment. In the [YEAR] school year, [NUMBER] students in [STATE] received corporal punishment according to the US Department of Education's Civil Rights Data Collection. [DISPARITY SENTENCE WITH FIGURE, IF VERIFIED FOR THE STATE.]

Why this bill: [TWO SENTENCES ON WHAT THE BILL DOES.]

What the evidence says: [THREE SENTENCES, EACH WITH A CITATION FROM facts/claims/. Use the AAP 2018 policy statement, the Gershoff & Grogan-Kaylor 2016 meta-analysis, and the state's own CRDC numbers.]

What replaces it: [NUMBER] of our [TOTAL] districts have already ended the practice. They rely on school-wide positive behavior supports and restorative practices, which are already in the state's [MTSS / discipline framework] and supported by federal Title IV-A funds. A free, open implementation guide is available at [training/ URL].

The cost of inaction: liability. [CITE A SETTLEMENT OR SUIT IN THE STATE OR CIRCUIT FROM data/states/<XX>.yaml OR strategy/case-studies/.]

I urge the committee to report [BILL NUMBER] favorably.

Respectfully,
[NAME]
[CONTACT]
```
