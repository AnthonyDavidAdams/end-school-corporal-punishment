POLICY: PROHIBITION OF CORPORAL PUNISHMENT

Policy Number: [NUMBER]
Adopted: [DATE]
[DISTRICT NAME] School District

I. PURPOSE
The Board of Education recognizes that maintaining a safe, supportive learning environment requires effective discipline strategies that do not rely on physical punishment.

II. POLICY STATEMENT
Corporal punishment is prohibited in all schools and programs operated by the [DISTRICT NAME] School District. No employee, volunteer, or contractor shall inflict corporal punishment on any student.

III. DEFINITION
Corporal punishment means the intentional infliction of physical pain upon a student's body as a disciplinary measure. This includes, but is not limited to: paddling, spanking, slapping, or any other form of physical punishment.

IV. EXCEPTIONS
This policy does not prohibit the use of reasonable physical intervention necessary to:
- Protect a student or others from imminent physical harm
- Obtain possession of a weapon or dangerous object
- Protect property from serious damage
- Maintain order when other means have failed

V. ALTERNATIVE DISCIPLINE STRATEGIES
The District shall provide training to all staff on positive behavioral interventions and supports (PBIS), restorative practices, and other evidence-based discipline strategies.

VI. VIOLATIONS
Any employee who violates this policy shall be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

VII. REPORTING
Any incident involving physical contact with a student for discipline purposes must be documented and reported to the building principal within 24 hours.

Approved by the Board of Education on [DATE].
