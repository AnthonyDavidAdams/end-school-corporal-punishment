```
AN ACT relating to student discipline in public schools.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF [STATE]:

SECTION 1. PROHIBITION OF CORPORAL PUNISHMENT

(a) No person employed by or volunteering for a public school district shall inflict or cause to be inflicted corporal punishment upon a student.

(b) For purposes of this section, "corporal punishment" means the deliberate infliction of physical pain as a means of discipline, including but not limited to:
    (1) Paddling, spanking, or hitting;
    (2) Slapping or striking;
    (3) Any other form of physical punishment.

(c) This section shall not prohibit:
    (1) The use of reasonable physical force necessary to maintain order and control in a school or classroom setting;
    (2) The use of reasonable physical force necessary to quell a disturbance threatening physical injury to persons or damage to property;
    (3) The use of reasonable physical force necessary for self-defense or the defense of others;
    (4) The use of reasonable physical force necessary to obtain possession of a weapon or other dangerous object within a student's control;
    (5) The use of reasonable physical force necessary to protect a student from self-harm.

SECTION 2. ENFORCEMENT

(a) Each school district shall adopt policies prohibiting corporal punishment consistent with this Act within 90 days of the effective date.

(b) Violations of this section may result in disciplinary action against the employee, up to and including termination.

(c) Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit a parent's right to pursue civil remedies.

SECTION 3. EFFECTIVE DATE

This Act shall take effect on [DATE].
```
