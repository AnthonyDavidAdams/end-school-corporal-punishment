# Letter to School Principal

Placeholders are in [BRACKETS]. Facts cited in this template are maintained in `facts/claims/`; check `facts/README.md` for the current figures before sending.

```
Dear Principal [NAME],

I am the parent of [CHILD'S NAME], a student at [SCHOOL NAME].

I am writing to formally request that my child NOT receive corporal punishment under any circumstances. I do not consent to any school employee using physical discipline on my child.

Please place this letter in my child's file and ensure that all staff who interact with my child are aware of this request. If there are any behavioral issues, I ask that you contact me directly so we can work together on an appropriate response.

I am also requesting information about:
1. The school's current discipline policy
2. Any forms required to opt out of corporal punishment
3. The discipline procedures that will be used for my child instead

Please confirm receipt of this letter and let me know if you need any additional information.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Sincerely,
[YOUR NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[PHONE NUMBER]
[EMAIL]

Parent/Guardian of [CHILD'S NAME]
Student ID: [IF APPLICABLE]
```
