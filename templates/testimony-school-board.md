# Public comment at a school board meeting

Most boards allow three minutes. That is about 400 words. Say who you are, one number, one story that is yours to tell, and one ask.

```
Good evening. My name is [NAME]. I am a [parent of a student at SCHOOL / teacher / pediatrician / taxpayer] in this district.

I am here about corporal punishment. Our district's policy [POLICY CODE] still allows an employee to hit a student with a board. According to the federal Civil Rights Data Collection, [NUMBER] students in this district received corporal punishment in [YEAR]. [If applicable: Federal data also show that Black students and students with disabilities in this district were paddled at [X] times the rate of their classmates.]

[ONE STORY, 60 SECONDS: your child, your classroom, your practice. No other family's child.]

The American Academy of Pediatrics, the American Psychological Association, and the National Education Association all oppose this practice. [NUMBER] of the [TOTAL] districts in [STATE] have already ended it, including [NEIGHBORING DISTRICT], and their schools did not fall apart. They use the same positive-behavior and restorative approaches our teachers are already trained in.

I am asking the board to do three things. First, put a policy revision to end corporal punishment on next month's agenda. Second, until then, direct staff to honor every parent's written refusal without exception. Third, publish our discipline data by school on the district website every year.

Thank you. I have left copies of the data and a model policy with the clerk.
```

Bring: a one-page printout of the district's CRDC numbers (from `data/crdc/`), `templates/district-policy.md`, and the list of nearby districts that have banned it (from `data/districts/`).
