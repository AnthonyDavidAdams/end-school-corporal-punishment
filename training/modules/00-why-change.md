# Module 0: Why change, in the words of the schools that did

**Time:** 30 minutes. **Audience:** school board, full staff on the first in-service day.

## What this module replaces

The assumption that ending corporal punishment means losing control of the building.

## The evidence, briefly

See `../evidence.md` sections A1, A7, A12 and A13. The short version: a 2016 meta-analysis of parental spanking found it associated with more aggression and worse mental health later; on the short term the evidence is mixed, since that meta-analysis found no significant link with immediate defiance (d = .14; five small studies, 150 children) while an earlier one it cites linked physical punishment to more immediate compliance. The school-specific meta-analysis finds corporal punishment associated with worse behavior and lower achievement; no study has found that ending it increases misbehavior, and no study has found that using it improves behavior over time. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends that corporal punishment in all school settings be abolished in every state by law.

## The practice

Read aloud short summaries of two or three places that stopped, chosen to be as close to your district as possible, from the sourced case studies in `../../strategy/case-studies/`. They are accounts of statutes, regulations and board votes; first-person accounts from principals have not been collected yet. If a principal from a nearby district that stopped will speak or record a few minutes, use that instead. Then ask the staff three questions and write the answers on the wall:

1. What do we actually use corporal punishment for? (List the behaviors. It is usually the same five.)
2. Which of those behaviors does paddling stop for good? (It will be none.)
3. What are we afraid of if we stop?

The third list is the training plan. Modules 2 through 5 answer every item on it.

## Handout

A one-page sheet: the district's own numbers from `../../data/crdc/`, the list of neighboring districts that have ended the practice from `../../data/districts/`, and the 2023 AAP school statement in one paragraph (`../../facts/claims/org-aap-2023-school-statement.md`).

## When it is not working

If staff are hostile, do not argue the evidence. Ask the second question again and wait. Then ask whether the students paddled most often were the same students, year after year, and let the room answer.
