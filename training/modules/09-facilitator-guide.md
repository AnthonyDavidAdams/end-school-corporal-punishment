# Module 9: Facilitator guide and slide notes

## Who should run this

A principal from a district that has already made the switch is the best facilitator. Second best: the district's own leadership team using this guide. Outside consultants are not required and, in the communities this is written for, often hurt.

## Sequence and time

| When | What | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Board meeting | Module 0 (30 min) plus the policy vote | 1 |
| First in-service day | Module 0 for all staff, then Module 2 | 4 |
| Week 2 | Module 3, leadership team; ladder posted by Friday | 3 |
| Week 4 | Module 4, all staff | 3 |
| Week 6 | Module 7 parent letter out; parent meeting | 2 |
| Week 8 | Module 5 and Module 8, leadership team | 4 |
| Week 10 | Module 6, all staff | 2 |
| Day 90 | Policy takes effect; baseline numbers published | |

About 14 hours per teacher across the semester, plus the weekly 20 minutes for the leadership team, as `01-transition-plan.md` says.

## Slide notes

There is no slide deck and there should not be a long one. Each module's practice section fits on three slides: what it replaces, the practice, the script. The handouts are the takeaway. If a slide has a citation, it comes from `../evidence.md` with the section letter.

## Ground rules for the facilitator

1. Never argue the research with a hostile room. Ask Module 0's second question and wait.
2. Never say the old practice was abuse. Many people in the room administered it in good faith; some were paddled and love the teachers who did it. Say it did not work and this does.
3. Name the fear. "You are worried the building will be chaos. Here is what we will publish every month so you can see whether it is."
4. Give people the script, not the theory. Teachers want to know what to say at 8:05 on the first day.
5. Do the routines in the training. Run the Good Behavior Game with the staff. Practice the five questions in pairs. Nobody learns a script by reading it.

## Adapting for a very small school

A school with one administrator and no counselor runs the ladder with the principal and one lead teacher per grade band sharing rungs 1 and 2. Check-In/Check-Out can be done by the secretary, the custodian, or a coach; the research does not require a counselor, it requires a consistent adult.

## Review

This curriculum is a draft. The `training-review` task in `../../tasks/README.md` asks people who have run a school to review one module at a time against `../evidence.md` and file issues. The modules change when the reviews say so.
