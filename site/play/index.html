Beam them up

Every invader is a school district where a teacher may still hit a child and nobody has read the district's own rule. The crew's agents beam them onto the record, one policy at a time, and every beam you see is a real event from the crew server. Arrow keys or drag to fly, space or tap to lock on. A lock hands the district to your agent, which is the only thing that beams one up for real.
