Arkansas

Legal in public schools

Second-highest per-capita use (6.3 per 1,000 in 2021-22; 3,165 students). A 2025 thesis reportedly counts 171 districts (72 percent) allowing and 51 banning. The disability ban is Act 557 of 2019, not 'Act 1249 of 2021' as some lists say. No ban bill found 2025-26 (Arkansans Against School Paddling tracker).

The law

A.C.A. § 6-18-505(c) (as amended by Act 557 of 2019), § 6-18-503(b), § 6-17-112 text

Private schools are not covered.

Limits

  • No corporal punishment on a child who is intellectually disabled, non-ambulatory, non-verbal, or autistic (Act 557 of 2019, SB 381)
  • Only for cause, reasonable, after warning, by a teacher or administrator, in the presence of an administrator or designee (§ 6-18-503(b)); district must authorize it in its written discipline policy

Bills

  • SB 59 (Act 165) (2023), Sen. Linda Chesterfield: Expanded staff authority to use 'reasonable and appropriate physical force'; not a ban signed record
