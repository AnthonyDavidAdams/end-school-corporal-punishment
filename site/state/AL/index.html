Alabama

Legal in public schools

No statutory limits: no consent, disability, witness, instrument or record rules. Third-highest per-capita use (5.4 per 1,000 in 2021-22; 4,083 students; 4,352 in 2023-24, the only large-use state that rose). No ban bill found in 2023 or 2026; the 2024 and 2025 sessions could not be searched (site blocks automation).

The law

Ala. Code § 16-28A-1 (teachers may use corporal punishment 'as may be prescribed by the local board'; immunity if policy followed) text

Private schools are not covered.

No statutory limits: no consent requirement, no disability exemption, no witness or record rules.
