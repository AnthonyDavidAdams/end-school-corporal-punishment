Georgia

Legal in public schools

Near-dormant: a May 2026 Savannah Morning News review identified six districts with permitting policies (Wilcox, Laurens, Coffee, Tattnall, Appling, Heard; reported). 1,547 students in 2021-22; 1,127 in 2023-24. No ban bill in 2025-26.

The law

O.C.G.A. §§ 20-2-730 to 20-2-732 text

Private schools are not covered.

Limits

  • Not excessive or unduly severe; not a first-line punishment unless the pupil was warned; administered in the presence of a principal, assistant principal or designee informed beforehand in the pupil's presence; written explanation to the parent on request
  • No corporal punishment on a child whose parent filed, on the day of enrollment, a Georgia-licensed physician's statement that it would be detrimental to the child's mental or emotional stability (§ 20-2-731)

Bills

  • HB 356 (2023), Reps. Debra Bazemore, James Beverly, Sam Park, Carolyn Hugley (D): Prohibit corporal punishment in any public school; died after second reading failed record
