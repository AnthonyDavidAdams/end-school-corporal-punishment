Mississippi

Legal in public schools

Highest per-capita use in the nation: 3,678 students in 2021-22 (8.3 per 1,000), 20,309 in 2017-18. Students with disabilities struck fell from 3,304 to 30 after the 2019 law, though Mississippi Today reported 211 paddlings of students with disabilities after the ban. Twelve bills in four sessions, three per year from the same three sponsors (a Democratic full ban in each chamber and a Republican opt-in), have all died in committee without a hearing. February 2025: an East Jasper third-grader's arm was broken during a paddling the mother had approved; no action found.

The law

Miss. Code Ann. § 37-11-57 (as amended by HB 1182, 2019, Laws 2019 ch. 354) text

Private schools are not covered.

Limits

  • No corporal punishment on any student with a disability (IEP or 504 plan), with no immunity for violations (effective July 1, 2019). No consent, witness, instrument or record requirements in statute

Bills

  • HB 306 (2026), Rep. Carl Mickens (D): Full ban; died in committee Feb 3, 2026 failed record
  • HB 1187 (2026), Rep. Carolyn Crawford (R): Parental opt-in and annual notice; died in committee Feb 3, 2026 failed record
  • SB 2296 (2026), Sen. Sollie Norwood (D): Full ban; died in committee Feb 3, 2026 failed record
  • HB 269 / HB 794 / SB 2614 (2025), Mickens (ban), Crawford (opt-in), Norwood (ban): All died in committee without a hearing failed record
  • HB 70 / HB 1514 / SB 2375 (2024), Mickens, Crawford, Norwood: All died in committee failed record
  • HB 158 / HB 291 / SB 2577 (2023), Mickens, Crawford, Norwood: All died in committee failed record
