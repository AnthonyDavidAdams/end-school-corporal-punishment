Arizona

Legal in public schools

Districts set their own rules; 72 students in 2021-22, 202 in 2023-24. A ban bill has been filed every year since 2023 and never received a hearing.

The law

A.R.S. § 15-843(B)(2) (boards prescribe 'procedures for using corporal punishment if allowed by the governing board') text

Private schools are not covered.

No statutory limits: no consent requirement, no disability exemption, no witness or record rules.

Bills

  • SB1594 (2026), Sen. Lela Alston (D): Prohibit corporal punishment by any district or charter employee; second read Feb 4, 2026, no further action failed record
  • SB1478 (2025), Sens. Alston, Burch, Epstein, Kuby (D): Prohibit corporal punishment; died without a hearing failed record
  • SB1379 (2024), Sen. Lela Alston (D) + 12: Prohibit corporal punishment; died without a hearing failed record
  • HB2071 (2023), Rep. Laura Terech (D) + 14: Prohibit corporal punishment; died without a hearing failed record
