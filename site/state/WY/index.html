Wyoming

Legal in public schools

The statute grants immunity rather than authorizing the practice. Press in 2022-23 reported one to three of 48 districts still had it in policy (Niobrara County SD #1 named) and no incidents reported federally since 2012; Laramie County SD 1 reaffirmed its prohibition in October 2025. Advocate: principal Jeff Jones.

The law

W.S. § 21-4-308(b) (immunity for reasonable corporal discipline authorized by board policy) text

Private schools are not covered.

No statutory limits: no consent requirement, no disability exemption, no witness or record rules.

Bills

  • SF0047 (2023 General Session), Joint Education Interim Committee: Repeal immunity for corporal discipline; Senate 26-5, House Committee of the Whole failed 12-47 on February 14, 2023 failed record
  • HB0065 (2026 Budget Session), Joint Education Interim Committee: Require district discipline policies, parental notice and reporting; repeal the corporal-discipline immunity; failed introduction 38-24 (two-thirds needed) February 9, 2026 failed record
