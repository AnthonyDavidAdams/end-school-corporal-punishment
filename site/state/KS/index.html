Kansas

Legal in public schools

The state education department's general counsel (May 2025): 'there are no state laws that address corporal punishment' for K-12. The Kansas Association of School Boards model policy JDA says corporal punishment 'shall not be permitted,' adopted by many districts. Zero students reported in 2017-18, 2020-21 and 2021-22. No bill 2023-26. Kept as 'legal' because nothing prohibits it; effectively unused.

The law

No school corporal punishment statute; only K.S.A. 21-5602 (criminal child abuse, 'cruel and inhuman corporal punishment') text

Private schools are not covered.

No statutory limits: no consent requirement, no disability exemption, no witness or record rules.
