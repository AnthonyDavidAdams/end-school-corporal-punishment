Indiana

Legal in public schools

No law explicitly bans or allows it; 8 students reported in 2021-22, 16 in 2017-18. SB 531's inclusion of nonpublic schools confirms no existing private-school ban.

The law

No corporal punishment statute; IC 20-33-8-8(b) (school personnel 'stand in the relation of parents' and may take 'any disciplinary action necessary') text

Private schools are not covered.

No statutory limits: no consent requirement, no disability exemption, no witness or record rules.

Bills

  • SB 531 (2025), Sen. J.D. Ford (D): Ban corporal punishment in public, charter and state-accredited nonpublic schools; no hearing failed record
