One sentence.

For Missouri school board members: what the law permits, what the research finds, where the protection for staff stops, and the one sentence that ends it.

The four-minute film, Texas edition: the research and the ask are the same everywhere; the Missouri figures are below. A Missouri edition is in production.

“Corporal punishment is strictly prohibited as a method of discipline.”

28 Missouri boards already use this sentence, in policy JGA-2. For a district that permits corporal punishment on paper and has not used it in years, adopting the sentence changes nothing on Monday morning except the exposure. In Missouri, 72 districts are in exactly that position.

Missouri in numbers

1,002 students were struck in Missouri public schools in 2023-24. In 2017-18 it was 2,461: the practice is already leaving. Of the students struck, 86 percent were boys and 16 percent had a disability.

U.S. Department of Education Civil Rights Data Collection.

What the research finds

Across 21 studies and 67,400 children, school corporal punishment is associated with more aggressive and disruptive behavior, more anxiety and depression, and lower school performance. Across 75 studies it did not even reliably stop the behavior in the moment. Every source is at Facts.

What Missouri law says

RSMo 160.261 (as amended by SBs 681 and 662, 2022) permits it. The law's own conditions: No pupil may be subject to corporal punishment without the parent being notified and providing written permission (opt-in, effective August 28, 2022); Spanking must be administered by certificated personnel in the presence of a district-employee witness to be exempt from the abuse definition (160.261.10); Each board's written discipline policy must state its determination on corporal punishment. Every immunity statute runs out at the policy's edge: where the policy is not followed, the protection is gone, and whether it was followed is decided afterward, by someone else.

Who already did it

112 of the 324 Missouri boards on the public record prohibit it in writing. 28 of them use the sentence above. See every Missouri district's own policy line.

What we are asking

Put the sentence on an agenda. The two-page board brief for your district, the model policy in your state's numbering, a staff training and the letters to staff and families are free at the kit. When it is adopted, the district is recognized as a Safe School, with a certificate and a press release it can use.
