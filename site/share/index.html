Rules for every piece
  • Every number comes from facts/claims with its year; the claim id is on each post.
  • Sign every post earthpilot.org/kids.
  • No students: no photographs or renderings of children being struck.
  • No contempt for the South, faith, rural life, teachers, or parents who were paddled themselves.
  • One idea per post: a number, a line, a place to go.
