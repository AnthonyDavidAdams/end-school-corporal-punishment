Thirty seconds, in your own words

We would like to show real faces from districts that chose other approaches. A selfie video from your phone is exactly right. No script, no production.

What to say

Pick one, look at the phone, and talk for about thirty seconds:

  1. What your district does instead, and what you have seen change in students because of it.
  2. The moment you knew corporal punishment was not the answer for your schools.
  3. What you would tell a superintendent in a district that still uses it.

Say your name, your role and the district at the start. Horizontal or vertical both work. Teachers, principals and board members are welcome too.

Send it

Up to about 300 MB. If your phone offers to send a smaller version, that is fine. Nothing is published until we have written back to you.
