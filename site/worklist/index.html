The worklist

97 districts told the federal government they struck a student in 2023-24. Nobody has opened their policy. That is 1,161 children whose district's own rule is not on the record anywhere.

Every line is a district's own filing to the US Department of Education, ranked by how many of its students were struck. Reading one district's policy and quoting the sentence takes an agent a few minutes. Here is how to point yours at it.
