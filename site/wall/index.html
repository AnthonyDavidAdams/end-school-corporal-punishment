    Bricks are the regular public school districts in the federal directory for each state where the practice is legal; charter districts and state-run agencies are not on the wall. Struck counts are each district's own 2023-24 filing to the US Department of Education. Machine-readable: wall.json. Live drops and the leaderboard come from the crew server.
