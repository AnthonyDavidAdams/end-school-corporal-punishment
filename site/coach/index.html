Behavior Coach

A child is refusing, hitting, melting down, or not doing the work. Tell the coach what is happening and it gives you the one highest-leverage thing to do this week, the words to say, and what to watch.

It is built only on practices with published evidence: the classroom routines, the office response ladder, repair conversations and Check-In/Check-Out from the free Safe Discipline Program kit, and the American Academy of Pediatrics' guidance for parents. It will not suggest hitting a child, and it says when something needs a counselor, a pediatrician or a hotline instead of a plan.

Anonymous by design. The coach never asks for a name and the site keeps no record of the conversation; it lives in this tab and is gone when you close it. It is a coach, not a clinician or a lawyer. If a child is in danger, call 911. For thoughts of self-harm, call or text 988.
