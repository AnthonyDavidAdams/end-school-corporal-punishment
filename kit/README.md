# The Safe Discipline Program kit

Everything a district needs to end corporal punishment well: the policy, the motion, the words for
staff, families and students, the training, and the ninety days between the vote and the change.

Offered as an **in-kind grant funded and administered by EarthPilot.org** — no cost to the district,
no cash changes hands. Delivered with coaching for the superintendent and principals through the
first year, including the board presentation and the parent letter.

The position is the best one, not the easiest one: **prohibited entirely.** Not "with parental
consent," not "as a last resort." The evidence base (`../training/evidence.md`), the American Academy
of Pediatrics (2023), the Secretary of Education (2023), 137 of 199 countries, and every district in
Kentucky and North Carolina are on that side. A district that adopts consent-only is choosing to keep
hitting the children whose parents say yes, and the data say those are disproportionately the
children with the least protection.

## What is in the kit, in the order it is used

| | File | Used by | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Model policy](01-model-policy.md) | Board, superintendent, solicitor | First reading |
| 2 | [Board motion and resolution](02-board-motion.md) | Board president, any member | The vote |
| 3 | [Announcement to staff](03-announcement-staff.md) | Superintendent, principals | The week after the vote, before anything is public |
| 4 | [Letter to families](04-announcement-parents.md) | Superintendent | Same week |
| 5 | [Telling the students](05-announcement-students.md) | Principals, teachers | Same week, by grade band |
| 6 | [The history, in one page](06-history.md) | Everyone who has to answer "why now?" | Any time |
| 7 | [Training, transition, and what replaces the paddle](07-training-transition-alternatives.md) | Leadership team, all staff | Days 1 to 90 |

The training itself is the ten open modules in `../training/modules/` (about fourteen staff hours),
and the alternatives are Modules 2 through 5: classroom routines, the office response ladder, repair
conversations, and plans for the few students who keep coming back.

## Three rules for using it

1. **Say it was a mistake.** Not "times have changed." The announcements are written to acknowledge,
   without excuse, that the district hit children and should not have. Districts that skip this
   spend the next year relitigating it; districts that say it once are done with it.
2. **Never name a child, a family, or an employee** in any of these documents or in any meeting
   about them. The history is institutional.
3. **Cite only what is in `../facts/claims/`.** Every figure in the kit carries the id of the claim
   it comes from. Check `../facts/README.md` before printing; numbers move.

## Where the words came from

The acknowledgment language follows the districts and countries that ended the practice and said
so plainly rather than quietly (`intl-comparators-how-they-ended`,
`campaign-north-carolina-all-districts-2018`, `campaign-kentucky-zero-2023-24`). The alternatives
are the ones with randomized evidence (`alt-pbis-bradshaw-rcts`, `alt-restorative-rand-2018`,
`alt-good-behavior-game-kellam`), stated with their limits.
