claim: "In Tennessee, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: None. The Governmental Tort Liability Act's former procedural notice section (§ 29-20-302) is repealed. If a claim is denied, suit is in circuit court, only where the Act removes immunity. (T.C.A. §§ 29-20-305(a), 29-20-302 (repealed)); time limit to sue: Twelve months under the Governmental Tort Liability Act; one year for injuries to the person generally, two years if criminal charges are brought over the injury. (T.C.A. §§ 29-20-305(b), 28-3-104(a)); a child's age: Yes for the general limitation period: a person under eighteen may sue after legal rights are restored, within the limitation period, up to three years. The statute does not say whether this reaches the Act's twelve-month period; ask a lawyer. (T.C.A. § 28-1-106(a))."
