claim: "In Arizona, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. A written claim stating the facts and a settlement amount must be filed with the district within one hundred eighty days of when you knew of the injury, or it is barred. The claim is deemed denied after sixty days. (A.R.S. § 12-821.01); time limit to sue: One year for any action against a public entity or employee; two years for personal injury generally. (A.R.S. §§ 12-821, 12-542); a child's age: Partly. A minor may file the claim within one hundred eighty days after turning eighteen; the general tolling rule is separate. (A.R.S. §§ 12-821.01(D), 12-502)."
