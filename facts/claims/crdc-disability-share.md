The old site's 'students with disabilities are punished at twice the rate' is not supported nationally by any recent year. The disparity is real at the district level (Gershoff and Font 2016) and in specific states, and it moved with policy: Mississippi's count of students with disabilities struck fell from 3,304 in 2017-18 to 30 in 2021-22 after HB 1182 (2019). Students served only under Section 504 remain over-represented (5 percent of students struck versus 3 percent of enrollment in 2020-21). Use the state-level figures in data/crdc/ for testimony.
