Our own count from the district file. Because RSMo 160.261.1 has required a parent's written permission for any corporal punishment since August 28, 2022, every Missouri district that permits it is in practice an opt-in district; the 46 "allows" entries are policies that do not themselves recite the consent rule. The modal prohibition sentence among Missouri boards, used by 28 of them in policy JGA-2, is "Corporal punishment is strictly prohibited as a method of discipline." (data/states/model-language.json). Re-run the tally before quoting; the records request campaign adds entries weekly.
