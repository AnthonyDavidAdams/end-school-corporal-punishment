claim: "In South Carolina, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Optional. A verified claim may be filed with the district within one year; it is not required, but filing it extends the time to sue from two years to three. (S.C. Code § 15-78-80); time limit to sue: Two years under the Tort Claims Act, three if a verified claim was filed; three years generally. (S.C. Code §§ 15-78-110, 15-3-530(5)); a child's age: Yes, and the statute names the Tort Claims Act. The years under eighteen do not count, but the suit must be filed within one year after the child turns eighteen. (S.C. Code § 15-3-40)."
