The Catholic News Agency article (published 2011-03-08, read 2026-10-29 at its EWTN News address) quotes the archbishop as above, says the school had used a wooden paddle since 1951, and attributes to Dr. Monica Applewhite the statement that St. Augustine was the last Catholic school in the country to use one. The archdiocese's own statement and the Clarion Herald (the archdiocesan newspaper, which ceased publication in 2025) were not found online; press summaries also quote the archbishop saying the archdiocese had "for decades not permitted corporal punishment in its schools," which was not read in a primary source. The Christian Century item was not opened; it is listed as the lead for the June 2011 outcome (the Josephite order ending the practice). St. Augustine is run by the Josephites, not the archdiocese.
