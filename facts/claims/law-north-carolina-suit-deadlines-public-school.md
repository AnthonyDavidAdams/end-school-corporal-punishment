The board's immunity is waived only to the extent of its liability insurance. (G.S. 115C-42, 143-300.1); time limit to sue: Three years. (G.S. 1-52(5), (16)); a child's age: Yes. A child has the full three years after turning eighteen. (G.S. 1-17(a))."
