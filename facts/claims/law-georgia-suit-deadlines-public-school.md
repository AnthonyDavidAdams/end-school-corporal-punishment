claim: "In Georgia, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: None found. The six-month municipal notice and the twelve-month county and state rules do not name school districts, and the state Tort Claims Act excludes them. Separately, a teacher who paddles in good faith under board policy is not liable in a civil or criminal action. (O.C.G.A. §§ 36-33-5, 50-21-22(5), 20-2-732); time limit to sue: Two years for injuries to the person. (O.C.G.A. § 9-3-33); a child's age: Yes. A child has the same two years after turning eighteen. (O.C.G.A. § 9-3-90(b))."
