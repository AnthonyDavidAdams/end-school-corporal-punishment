claim: "In Oklahoma, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. A written claim filed with the clerk of the school board within one year of the loss, or it is forever barred; suit within one hundred eighty days after the claim is denied, or deemed denied at ninety days. The Act also exempts the use of necessary and reasonable force by a school employee to control and discipline a student. (51 O.S. §§ 156, 157, 155(35)); time limit to sue: Under the Governmental Tort Claims Act, the one-year claim and then one hundred eighty days. Generally two years for injury, one year for assault and battery. (51 O.S. §§ 156, 157; 12 O.S. § 95(A)); a child's age: Not for the claim. The Act pauses the notice deadline only for up to ninety days of incapacity from the injury. The general rule giving a child one year after the disability ends does not mention the Act. (51 O.S. § 156(E); 12 O.S. § 96)."
