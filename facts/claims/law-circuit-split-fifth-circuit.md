Substantive due process: Hall v. Tawney (4th Cir. 1980), Garcia v. Miera (10th Cir. 1987), Webb v. McCullough (6th Cir. 1987), Metzger v. Osbeck (3d Cir. 1988), Wise v. Pea Ridge (8th Cir. 1988, 855 F.2d 560), Neal v. Fulton County (11th Cir. 2000), Johnson v. Newburgh (2d Cir. 2001). Fourth Amendment: Wallace v. Batavia (7th Cir. 1995), Doe v. Hawaii DOE (9th Cir. 2003). Fifth Circuit: Fee v. Herndon (1990), T.O. v. Fort Bend ISD (2021, cert denied June 13, 2022), S.B. v. Jefferson Parish (2023, cert denied January 8, 2024 on an Institute for Justice petition). Strategy consequence: in the three states with the most paddling, the federal liability threat is the weakest in the country; liability arguments there must rest on state tort law, criminal law, and insurance, not on section 1983.
