All URLs verified live on 2026-09-09. The 2023 Guiding Principles names the Good Behavior Game, trauma-informed practices, SEL, restorative practices and PBIS as more effective alternatives. OSEP's 2016 letter states that an IEP team must consider PBIS when a child's behavior impedes learning (34 CFR 300.324(a)(2)(i)).
