The letter is the only executive-branch action on school corporal punishment to date. It counts 23 states that expressly allow (16) or do not prohibit (7) the practice, a broader definition than the 17-state count used on the map. The disparity ratios are from the 2017-18 Civil Rights Data Collection as quoted in the letter; the CRDC claims in this registry carry the underlying figures. The letter also cites nearly 900 preschoolers receiving corporal punishment.
