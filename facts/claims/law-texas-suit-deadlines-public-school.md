claim: "In Texas, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. The district is entitled to written notice within six months of the incident, describing the injury, the time and place, and the incident; not required if the district has actual notice of the injury. To sue a professional employee personally: written notice ninety days before suit and exhaustion of the district's grievance process. The Tort Claims Act does not reach school districts except for motor vehicles, or any intentional tort, including disciplinary action by school authorities. (Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §§ 101.101, 101.051, 101.057; Educ. Code §§ 22.0513, 22.0514); time limit to sue: Two years for personal injury. (Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003(a)); a child's age: Yes for the suit: time under eighteen is not counted. The six-month notice statute says nothing about minors. (Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.001)."
