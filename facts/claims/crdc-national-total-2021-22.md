This is the newest year with statistics published by the Office for Civil Rights ('approximately 24,500' in the January 2025 First Look; 24,534 from the estimations API, non-IDEA plus with-disabilities measures). The count is unduplicated students, not incidents: the same file records 31,779 instances, about 1.3 per student. The Department itself says the practice is 'likely underreported.' The old site's '~70,000 students in 2022' conflated the 2017-18 figure (69,492) with a later year; do not use it.
