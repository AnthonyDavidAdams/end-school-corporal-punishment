claim: "In Arkansas, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: None in the immunity statute. School districts are immune from suit for damages except to the extent they carry liability insurance, and no tort action lies against the district for its employees' acts; the claim is against the insurance. (Ark. Code § 21-9-301); time limit to sue: Three years is the period in § 16-56-105; the specific item covering injury to the person could not be read on the official site, so confirm it with a lawyer. (Ark. Code § 16-56-105); a child's age: Yes. A person under twenty-one when the claim accrues may sue within three years after reaching full age. (Ark. Code § 16-56-116(a))."
