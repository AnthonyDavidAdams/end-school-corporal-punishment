claim: "In Louisiana, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: None. The Governmental Claims Act requires no pre-suit notice, and a school board is a political subdivision under it. Suit must be in a Louisiana state court, and damages for one person are capped at five hundred thousand dollars. (La. R.S. 13:5102(B)(1), 13:5106); time limit to sue: Two years for injuries on or after July 1, 2024; one year for earlier injuries. (La. Civ. Code art. 3493.1); a child's age: No. Prescription runs against minors. The suspension between parents and children during minority concerns a child's claims against the parents, not a school. (La. Civ. Code arts. 3468, 3469)."
