Stat. § 768.28(9)(a) (general employee immunity; the school statutes have no corporal punishment immunity clause)); the statute's consent rule is: Opt-in. A board that allows it must require parental consent, for the year or before each paddling (§ 1002.20(4)(c), since July 1, 2025)."
