Verbatim, 300.153(c): "The complaint must allege a violation that occurred not more than one year prior to the date that the complaint is received in accordance with § 300.151." 300.152(a): "Each SEA must include in its complaint procedures a time limit of 60 days after a complaint is filed under § 300.153 to" carry out an investigation if needed, take the complainant's information, review the information and issue a written decision. 300.153(d): the complainant must send a copy to the district at the same time. Used in the parent guide for a child with an IEP.
