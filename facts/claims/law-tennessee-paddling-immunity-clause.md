claim: "In Tennessee, no immunity clause in the corporal punishment sections. Before any paddling the teacher or principal must document in the student's record that they addressed the behavior, gave consequences, consulted the parent and considered a disability evaluation (§ 49-6-4103(a)(2)). (T.C.A. §§ 49-6-4103, 49-6-4104); the statute's consent rule is: None for most students. For a student with a disability, prohibited unless district policy allows it and the parent gives written permission stating the type and circumstances (§ 49-6-4103(b))."
