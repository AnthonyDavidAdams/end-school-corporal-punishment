Both halves are accurate and the stronger causal claim is not supported. The 2023 AAP statement and Gershoff 2017 argue from the absence of any evidence of benefit plus the harm evidence. The training says exactly this and pairs abolition with PBIS and the Good Behavior Game so that paddling is not simply swapped for suspension. A district that publishes referral counts before and after its switch (training module 8) would produce the first such evidence.
