Sovereign immunity is waived only for motor vehicles and dangerous property conditions, and only to the extent of insurance. (RSMo 537.600, 537.610); time limit to sue: Two years for assault and battery; five years for other injury to the person. (RSMo 516.140, 516.120(4)); a child's age: Yes. A person under twenty-one when the claim accrues has the full period after the disability is removed. (RSMo 516.170)."
