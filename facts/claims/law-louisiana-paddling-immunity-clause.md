Should any such teacher, principal, or administrator be cast in judgment for damages in such suit, it shall be the obligation of the school board employing such defendant to indemnify him fully against such judgment [...]\" (La. The immunity runs only as far as the board's policy: outside the policy (no witness or warning where required, no consent form where required, a protected student, excessive force) the statute's protection does not apply.
