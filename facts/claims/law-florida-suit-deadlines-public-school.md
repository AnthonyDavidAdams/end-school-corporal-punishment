claim: "In Florida, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. A written claim to the agency and to the Department of Financial Services within three years, and a denial, before suit. The statute does not name school boards; a lawyer must confirm how it applies to a district. (Fla. Stat. § 768.28(6)); time limit to sue: Four years for a claim under the sovereign immunity statute; generally two years for negligence and four for battery. (Fla. Stat. §§ 768.28(14), 95.11); a child's age: Rarely. Minority pauses the clock only while the child has no parent or guardian able to sue, and never beyond seven years. Nothing in § 768.28 pauses the claim deadline. (Fla. Stat. § 95.051(1)(i))."
