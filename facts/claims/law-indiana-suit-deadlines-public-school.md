claim: "In Indiana, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. Written notice to the school board (and the state political subdivision risk management commission, where it applies) within one hundred eighty days of the loss, or the claim is barred; suit only after denial. A separate pre-suit notice applies to suits against public schools. (Ind. Code §§ 34-13-3-8, 34-13-3-13, 34-13-3.5); time limit to sue: Two years for injury to the person. (Ind. Code § 34-11-2-4(a)); a child's age: For the suit, yes: two years after the disability ends. For the notice, only an incapacitated person gets one hundred eighty days after the incapacity ends; ask a lawyer whether a child qualifies. (Ind. Code §§ 34-11-6-1, 34-13-3-9)."
