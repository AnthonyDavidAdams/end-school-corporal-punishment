An absence claim: each body's own site was searched on 2026-10-29 and the searches above are what was run. Absence in a site search is not proof that no action exists (a resolution could be indexed under other words, or held in a journal the search does not cover), so the booklet says "returns no resolution" and "the question has not come to the floor", not "the church is for it". The SBC search for "corporal punishment" returned twelve items (capital punishment, hell, prison reform, religious liberty, alcohol), none about children. The Episcopal archive's own search gives "No records were found which matched your query" for both phrases. The Brethren resolution is about school conflict-resolution instruction, media violence and gun legislation after Columbine. Not checked, or blocked: Assemblies of God position papers (ag.org returned 403), American Baptist Churches USA (site search did not run), Lutheran Church-Missouri Synod, Seventh-day Adventist, National Council of Churches. Re-run the searches before quoting this claim in a later year.
