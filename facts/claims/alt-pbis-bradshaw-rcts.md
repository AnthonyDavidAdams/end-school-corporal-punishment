Bradshaw, Mitchell and Leaf 2010 (Journal of Positive Behavior Interventions) and Bradshaw, Waasdorp and Leaf 2012 (Pediatrics, 12,344 children). Effects depend on implementation fidelity; most RCT evidence is elementary. PBIS is a framework, not a package: adopting the name without tiers, data and coaching does not reproduce these results.
