A guest column by the bishop, read in the Yahoo syndication of The Oklahoman on 2026-10-29. Reported, not a denominational act: it is one bishop's statement in a newspaper, and the original at oklahoman.com was not opened. The column quotes the Book of Resolutions ("It is difficult to imagine Jesus of Nazareth condoning any action that is intended to hurt children") and the Social Principles ("corporal punishment models aggressive behavior as a solution to conflict"; "children must eventually develop their own conscience and self-discipline"). Use it as context for the Oklahoma debate and as a church leader's reading of the resolution in org-umc-2004-discipline-children-without-corporal-punishment, not as the church's position in itself.
