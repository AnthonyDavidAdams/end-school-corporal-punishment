Read at the archdiocesan schools policy site (a Google Sites manual on the archbaltschools.org domain) on 2026-10-29. Verbatim: "Catholic schools' personnel may never use corporal punishment." The page shows "May 2025" at the foot, not labeled as an adoption or revision date. Maryland prohibits corporal punishment in public schools, so, as with Des Moines, this is an example of a diocesan rule and not a rule inside a permitting state. Dioceses in permitting states (Mobile, Jackson, Galveston-Houston, Little Rock, Dallas, Oklahoma City) were searched on 2026-10-29 and no published policy text was found; ask the diocesan schools offices directly before saying anything about them.
