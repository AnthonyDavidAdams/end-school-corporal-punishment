Use this instead of the old line 'one of only two developed nations (with Singapore).' That line fails: Singapore permits caning of boys but is not an OECD member, and Australia's Queensland gap makes Australia the other OECD exception. Canada is marked prohibited in the table, but the Global Initiative's own Canada report notes Alberta and Manitoba lack a statutory prohibition, so even 'prohibited' countries have gaps. If Singapore must be mentioned: 'Among high-income countries, only the United States, Singapore and parts of Australia still permit teachers to hit children as punishment.'
