UK: Education (No. 2) Act 1986 s.47, extended to private schools in England and Wales by the 1998 Act s.131, Scotland 2000, Northern Ireland 2003; the Commons opposition in 1986 argued it 'should be a local decision for governing bodies.' Canada: Canadian Foundation for Children, Youth and the Law v. Canada, 2004 SCC 4. New Zealand s.139A and South Korea's 2011 decree are reported from secondary sources; verify the statute pages before quoting dates precisely.
