The 2011 statute left the decision to local boards but required a witness, written parental notice, records, an annual report to the State Board by race, gender and disability, and a written parental opt-out at enrollment. NC Child led two decades of advocacy; Robeson County ended the practice in April 2018 and Graham County (34 incidents at Robbinsville High in 2016-17) followed unanimously. 2025 ban bills HB 983 and S714 were parked in Rules committees. The map shows North Carolina as 'partial'.
