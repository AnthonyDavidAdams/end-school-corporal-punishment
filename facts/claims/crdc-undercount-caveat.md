Repeat this whenever a figure is quoted. Evidence of error in both directions: six jurisdictions that ban the practice reported nonzero counts in 2017-18, and a New Jersey district reported 380 students in 2023-24. Instances exceed students by about 30 percent (31,779 versus 24,534 in 2021-22).
