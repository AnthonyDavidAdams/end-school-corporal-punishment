Do not list the AFT among organizations that oppose the practice on the strength of a resolution. The 2023 POSSA endorsement (see law-federal-possa-2025, Bonamici press release) is the citable AFT position. The NEA is commonly reported as opposing corporal punishment in its annual resolutions, but the resolution text could not be retrieved; confirm the resolution number from the current NEA Handbook before citing. The American Bar Association's 1985 resolution could not be located either.
