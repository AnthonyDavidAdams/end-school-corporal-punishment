W.S. § 21-4-308(b). By 2022-23, press reported only one to three of Wyoming's 48 districts still had corporal punishment in policy (Niobrara County School District #1 named), and the state had reported no incidents to the federal government since 2012; the 2022 and 2023 district counts conflict. Laramie County SD 1 reaffirmed its prohibition in October 2025. The advocate was a middle-school principal, Jeff Jones, whose dissertation reviewed all 48 policies.
