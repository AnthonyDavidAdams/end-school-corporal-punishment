Maryland's ban works through the State Board's certificate of approval for nonpublic schools and exempts schools operated by a bona fide church organization. Colorado's 2023 statute is public-only despite a Global Initiative note suggesting otherwise. Dates for New Jersey (1867) and Iowa (1989) are reported from press, not verified on a statute page.
