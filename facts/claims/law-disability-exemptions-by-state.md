Mississippi HB 1182 amended § 37-11-57 for students with an IEP or 504 plan, effective July 1, 2019. Tennessee HB 2330 (Pub. Ch. 900) covers IEP and 504 students except where district policy allows and a parent gives written permission, effective July 1, 2018. Louisiana Act 266 (HB 79, 2017) covers certain students with exceptionalities, effective August 1, 2017. Oklahoma SB 364 covers any student identified with a disability under IDEA, replacing a 2017 law limited to the most significant cognitive disabilities with an IEP or parental-waiver exception. Kentucky's 704 KAR 7:170 also excluded IEP and 504 students before every district banned the practice.
