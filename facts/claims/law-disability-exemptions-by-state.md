Mississippi HB 1182 amended § 37-11-57 for students with an IEP or 504 plan, effective July 1, 2019. Tennessee HB 2330 (Pub. Ch. 900) covers IEP and 504 students except where district policy allows and a parent gives written permission, effective July 1, 2018. Louisiana Act 266 (HB 79, 2017) covers certain students with exceptionalities, effective August 1, 2017. Oklahoma SB 364 covers any student identified with a disability under IDEA, replacing a 2017 law limited to the most significant cognitive disabilities with an IEP or parental-waiver exception. Arkansas Act 557 of 2019 (SB 381) covers children who are intellectually disabled, non-ambulatory, non-verbal or autistic; it is sometimes miscited as Act 1249 of 2021. North Carolina has no statutory disability ban despite claims to the contrary. Kentucky's 704 KAR 7:170 also excluded IEP and 504 students before every district banned the practice.
