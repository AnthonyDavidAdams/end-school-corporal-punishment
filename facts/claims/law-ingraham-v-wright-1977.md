Powell for the Court; White dissenting with Brennan, Marshall and Stevens. The Court did acknowledge a 'constitutionally protected liberty interest' in freedom from bodily punishment (at 674) and left the substantive due process question open (at 659 n.12), which is where fifty years of circuit litigation have run.
