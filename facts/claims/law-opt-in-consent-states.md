Texas, North Carolina and Georgia (physician letter only) remain opt-out states, where the burden is on the parent to object. Missouri: RSMo 160.261, SBs 681 and 662, effective August 28, 2022. Louisiana: R.S. 17:416.1(B), Act 268 of 2023 (HB 242), effective August 1, 2023, House 74-21, Senate 37-1. Florida: § 1002.20(4)(c), ch. 2025-110, effective July 1, 2025, final votes unanimous. Opt-in is the parental-rights bill and passes where bans fail; it also produces a signed form that can be counted.
