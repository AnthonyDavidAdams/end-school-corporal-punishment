Per-capita rates were highest in Mississippi (8.3 per 1,000 enrolled), Arkansas (6.3) and Alabama (5.4). Sixteen states reported any use. The same four states were 75 percent in 2017-18 (when Mississippi led with 20,309) and 75 percent in our 2023-24 computation (when Alabama, at 4,352, was the only large-use state that rose).
