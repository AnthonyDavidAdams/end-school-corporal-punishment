2013-14 through 2017-18 from the OCR Part 4 factsheet (the 2013-14 figure was revised down from 'over 110,000' in Secretary King's 2016 letter). 2020-21 is not comparable: most schools were hybrid for part of the year and the survey skipped the item for virtual schools. Earlier: 223,190 in 2006-07 (HRW/ACLU from OCR data) and 'more than 160,000' in 2011-12 (Gershoff and Font 2016), both reported rather than verified on OCR pages.
