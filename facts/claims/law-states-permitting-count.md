Counts differ by source because states with no statute either way (Connecticut, Indiana, Kansas, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Maine) are classified differently: Cardona 2023 counted 23 (16 expressly allow, 7 do not prohibit); Bellwether 2024 said 17; APSAC 2025 said 18; NPR's August 2026 tracker said 15 allow and 30 plus DC ban. This project's list (data/states/) is: Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, Missouri, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Wyoming. No state enacted a full public-school ban in 2024, 2025, or 2026 (searched 2026-09-09); the most recent are Colorado and Idaho (2023).
