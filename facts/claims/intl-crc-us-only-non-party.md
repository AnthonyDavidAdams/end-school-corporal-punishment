The UN Treaty Collection page lists 140 signatories and 196 parties; the US signed on 16 February 1995 with no ratification. Somalia and South Sudan ratified in 2015. The 196 exceeds 193 UN members because non-member parties (Holy See, State of Palestine, Cook Islands, Niue) are included; confirm on the UN page if that detail is quoted.
