Kentucky Board of Education regulation 704 KAR 7:170 (effective August 30, 2022) required every district to either prohibit corporal punishment or permit it only with annual written consent, per-instance verbal consent, a same-gender certified witness, no instruments, counseling within a day, and exclusions for students with IEPs or 504 plans, homeless and foster youth. Every district chose prohibition. Statewide incidents fell from about 1,100 in 2011-12 to 142 in 2019-20 to 5 in 2022-23. Ban bills (2020 HB 22 passed the House 65-17 and died in the Senate; 2022 HB 119 died in committee) never became law; the statute still permits the practice, so the map shows Kentucky as 'partial'.
