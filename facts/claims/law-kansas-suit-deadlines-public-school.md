claim: "In Kansas, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. Written notice to the clerk or the school board before any suit; no suit until the claim is denied or one hundred twenty days pass. The notice has no deadline of its own, but the suit must still meet the limitation period, with at least ninety days after denial. (K.S.A. 12-105b(d), 12-105a); time limit to sue: Two years. (K.S.A. 60-513(a)(4)); a child's age: Yes for the suit: a child may sue within one year after turning eighteen, but never more than eight years after the act. The notice statute says nothing about minors. (K.S.A. 60-515(a))."
