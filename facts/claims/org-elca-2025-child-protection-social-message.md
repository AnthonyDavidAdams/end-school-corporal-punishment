Read from the church's PDF on 2026-10-29. Page 1: "This social message was adopted by the ELCA Church Council on November 12, 2025." Verbatim: "Worshiping communities are called to be safe spaces, free of violence, where physical intimidation or the use of force—including physical discipline—are specifically prohibited." And: "Synods and the churchwide organization are also encouraged to provide resources for parents and caregivers on effective forms of nonphysical discipline and theological resources that can equip the church to address child maltreatment". The message also describes physical punishment "such as spanking or hitting" as "often rooted in family and religious culture and tradition" in its section on physical abuse. A social message is adopted by the Church Council; it is a lower tier than a social statement, which needs a two-thirds vote of a churchwide assembly (elca.org). It is about church settings and parents; it does not name schools, so do not cite it as a position on school corporal punishment.
