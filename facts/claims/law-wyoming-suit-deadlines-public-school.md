claim: "In Wyoming, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. An itemized written claim, signed under oath, filed at the district's business office within two years of the act; suit within one year after the claim is filed. Governmental entities and employees are immune from any tort except the listed waivers. (W.S. §§ 1-39-113, 1-39-114, 1-39-104); time limit to sue: One year after the claim is filed. Generally four years for injury to rights and one year for assault or battery. (W.S. §§ 1-39-114, 1-3-105); a child's age: Only for the youngest. A child seven or younger has until the eighth birthday or two years after the act, whichever is longer. The general rule giving a minor three years after the disability ends does not mention the Act. (W.S. §§ 1-39-114, 1-3-114)."
