Read at umc.org on 2026-10-29. The page title carries "(DELETED/EXPIRED)"; the text shown is the 2016 Book of Resolutions version with the lines "ADOPTED 2004", "READOPTED 2012", "RESOLUTION #3088, 2008, 2012 BOOK OF RESOLUTIONS", "RESOLUTION #70, 2004 BOOK OF RESOLUTIONS", "See Social Principles, ¶ 162C". Two resolved clauses: members encouraged "to adopt discipline methods that do not include corporal punishment of their children"; congregations encouraged "to offer opportunities for dialogue and education on effective discipline of children." The whereas clauses say corporal punishment "models aggressive behavior as a solution to conflict" and that "It is difficult to imagine Jesus of Nazareth condoning any action that is intended to hurt children" (the latter as quoted by Bishop Nunn, org-umc-oklahoma-bishop-2024-column; the whereas text on the umc.org page was summarized, not quoted, by the fetch).

Status of the schools resolution: a publisher's table of contents for the 2020/2024 Book of Resolutions lists item 4212, "Prohibit Corporal Punishment in Schools and Childcare Facilities" (the 2004 original was Resolution 69, "Corporal Punishment in Schools and Child Care Facilities", calling on all states to enact laws prohibiting it). Its current text could not be read at umc.org on 2026-10-29 (several URL forms returned 404; the site search returns only the expired resolution). The General Board of Church and Society proposed petition #3087 to amend and readopt the family resolution with schools and childcare added for the 2024 General Conference; the outcome of that petition was not found. Say "adopted in 2004, readopted in 2012, now marked expired on the church's site" and no more.
