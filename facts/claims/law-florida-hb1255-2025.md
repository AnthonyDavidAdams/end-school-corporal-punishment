The consent provision came from a four-year effort by University of Florida students in the Florida Student Policy Forum, who first sought a ban for students with disabilities and settled for consent after pushback from conservative districts. Baseline: 17 Florida counties reported 516 instances in 2023-24 (Suwannee 74, Holmes 65, Calhoun 59, Columbia 50, Wakulla 45, Washington 43). Florida districts that still permit the practice are coded consent_required.
