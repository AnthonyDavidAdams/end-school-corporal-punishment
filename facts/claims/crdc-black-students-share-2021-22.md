Both-sexes figure computed from the OCR estimations API (6,438 of 24,534; enrollment 7,443,249 of 49,974,274), ratio 1.76 by share and 1.41 per capita against white students. Black-boys figure from the First Look, Figure 14. In 2017-18 the disparity was larger: 37.3 percent of students struck versus 15.1 percent of enrollment, which OCR summarized as 'Black students were 2.3 times more likely than white students.' The old site's '4x more likely' matches no national figure; the '4x' in the Cardona letter is Black girls versus white girls in 2017-18. Quote the group, the year, and the ratio together.
