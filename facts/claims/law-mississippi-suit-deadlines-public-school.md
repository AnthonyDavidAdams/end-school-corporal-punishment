claim: "In Mississippi, a family suing a public school district over a paddling faces these statutory clocks (read at the state's statute site on 2026-10-29): notice of claim: Yes. A notice of claim to the district's chief executive officer at least ninety days before suit; suit within one year of the incident, with the notice pausing the clock ninety-five days. Missing the window is an absolute bar. Read at Justia; the state's official code site could not be opened. (Miss. Code § 11-46-11); time limit to sue: One year under the Tort Claims Act; three years generally. (Miss. Code §§ 11-46-11(3), 15-1-49); a child's age: Yes. A child may bring the action within the time allowed after the disability is removed. The statute does not say whether the ninety-day notice is also deferred. (Miss. Code § 11-46-11(4))."
