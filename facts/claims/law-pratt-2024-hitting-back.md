A student note (the author was a 2024 J.D. candidate at the University of Florida Levin College of Law), not a faculty article; say so when citing. The sentence at 794: "And tort claims based on a reasonableness standard imply that some level of corporal punishment in schools is reasonable. This Note argues that all in-school corporal punishment is inherently unreasonable". Part II reviews Ingraham, the substantive due process circuits, Dobbs, and the Fourth Amendment route; Part III argues from article IX, section 1 (uniform education) and article I, section 23 (privacy) of the Florida constitution and closes with a call for legislation or a constitutional amendment. The abstract's count of "eighteen states" differs from this project's seventeen (law-states-permitting-count).
