# The look (Anthony's references, 2026-10-27)

Five references, one thread: paper. A torn-paper collage poster on cream stock with cut-paper block letters in black and red, typewriter
notes and red tape; a sheet of retro photo cut-outs on cream; a torn-newsprint collage cityscape with warm paper lamps; blue ballpoint
school doodles on crumpled yellow paper; a marker-doodled sketchbook cover. Tactile, handmade, limited palette.

Rules for every scene and every title card:
- Cream card stock is the world. Everything sits on it with a real shadow.
- Palette: cream, black, brick red, navy blue, kraft brown. Yellow only as light (windows, lamps, sun).
- Words are paper: black blocks with white condensed capitals, red strips with white, typewriter notes on torn scraps, a ballpoint doodle for accents. Never a clean digital caption.
- Scenes are cut-paper and torn-paper collage (the AI stills and Seedance clips); the edit is paper too (Remotion: crumpled paper, torn edges, tape).
- No legible text in generated images; all words are set in the edit so they can change per state.
- Tone: humane and plain. The subject is children being hit; the paper softens the medium, never the fact.
