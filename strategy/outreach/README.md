# Outreach to the people already doing this

Every organization and reporter in `../field.md` has been at this longer than we have. The first contact is not a pitch; it is an offer of what they do not have: an agent-maintained district dataset, a verified claims registry they can cite, a free replacement curriculum, and compute for the tasks they have never had staff for. Ask what they need before proposing anything.

## Rules

1. **One email per organization, from Anthony, in his voice.** Drafts are in `drafts/`; nothing is sent from this repository. Check the thread first: if Anthony has already corresponded with the person, the draft is wrong.
2. **Lead with their work.** Name the thing they did (the Kentucky regulation, the Texas map, the Colorado testimony) and the specific place it appears in this repository, with a link.
3. **Ask one question.** "What is the dataset or task you have wanted for years and never had the hands for?"
4. **Offer, do not recruit.** The repo is theirs to use under CC BY; we are not asking them to join anything.
5. **Log every contact** in `targets.yaml` with date and reply, so nobody writes twice.
6. **Journalists get facts, not asks.** Send the state table and the claims registry link; say a maintainer will check any figure they want within a day.

## Sequence

1. Researchers and the national organizations first (Gershoff, Holden and End Hitting USA, IDRA, NIECP): they can find errors fastest and their word carries in every state.
2. The state coalitions that won (Kentucky, North Carolina, Colorado, Florida students): ask each for a two-paragraph account for `../case-studies/` and permission to quote.
3. The state coalitions still fighting (Arkansas, Texas, Tennessee, Missouri): offer the district scan for their state first.
4. Reporters who have covered it, when there is a number to give them (the 2023-24 computed total is one).
5. Legislators' staff for the sponsors already on record (Riley, Allen, Alston, Nurrenbern, Talley, Rader, Cervania, Campbell): send the state file and the case studies, offer testimony support, ask nothing.

## Tooling

`targets.yaml` is the list. Send through whatever Anthony uses for personal email; do not use a bulk tool for this list. OpenSDR is for the district-by-district superintendent and board contact later, after the district scan, and only with the decision-maker CRM rules in `../../crm/README.md`.
