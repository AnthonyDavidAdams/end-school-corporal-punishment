# Oklahoma: the disability ban, 2023 to 2025

**2017:** HB 1623 (Laws 2017 ch. 222) barred corporal punishment of students with the most significant cognitive disabilities, with IEP and parental-waiver exceptions. Effective November 1, 2017.

**2023:** HB 1028 (Rep. John Talley, R) to bar it for all students with disabilities failed 45-43 on March 14, then passed 84-8 on reconsideration March 20 after members were pressed on the record, then stalled in the Senate (enacting clause stricken April 11; amended again in 2024; no floor vote).

**2025:** SB 364 (Sen. Dave Rader, Rep. Anthony Moore, R) passed the Senate 31-16 (February 25) and the House 63-25 (April 30) and became law without Gov. Kevin Stitt's signature on May 8, 2025 (Laws 2025 ch. 99, amending 70 O.S. § 13-116). It removes the prior IEP exception and the parental-consent waiver. The emergency clause failed; the effective date is either 90 days after sine die under the constitution or May 12, 2025 per one tracker. Flag when quoting.

**Opposition:** reported quotes citing Proverbs and parental rights from Sen. Shane Jett and Rep. Jim Olsen could not be sourced (the outlets blocked retrieval) and must not be published until read.

**Use:** 3,968 students in 2017-18 (American Indian/Alaska Native students 24 percent); 1,458 in 2021-22; 991 in 2023-24.

**Lesson:** a two-vote loss became an 84-8 win in six days once the vote was public; the Senate then took two more years. Republican sponsors carried every version with no outside coalition. The governor's refusal to sign or veto is the tell that opposition had become embarrassing rather than principled.
