# Case studies

Every documented win, loss and reversal, with the coalition, the argument that moved votes, the opposition's argument, the margin, and sources. Written so the next campaign can copy what worked. Add one after every board vote and hearing; losses are as valuable as wins.

| File | What happened | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| `colorado-2023.md` | State ban after 154 of 178 districts had already stopped | State ban, disability-rights framing |
| `idaho-2023.md` | Red-state ban bundled with restraint and seclusion after a newspaper investigation | State ban, Republican sponsor |
| `new-mexico-2011.md` | Narrow Democratic bill signed by a Republican governor as parental rights | State ban, parental-rights framing |
| `kentucky-regulation.md` | State board regulation made the practice administratively expensive; all 171 districts quit within one policy cycle | Regulatory route |
| `north-carolina-districts.md` | Opt-out and reporting statute plus twenty years of advocacy emptied the practice district by district | District-by-district |
| `florida-2025-consent.md` | Student-led campaign won opt-in consent unanimously after a disability ban was blocked | Narrowing |
| `oklahoma-2023-2025.md` | Disability ban failed by two votes, passed on reconsideration, stalled, then became law two years later without the governor's signature | Narrowing, persistence |
| `district-votes.md` | Robeson and Graham counties (wins); Overton ISD and Cassville (a non-change and a reversal) | District |
