# Federal attempts, 2010 to 2025

| Congress | Bill | Sponsor | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 111th | H.R. 5628, Ending Corporal Punishment in Schools Act (June 29, 2010), 17 cosponsors | Rep. Carolyn McCarthy (D-NY) | Referred to Education and Labor; a subcommittee hearing in April 2010 (record not located); never voted |
| 116th | H.R. 727 (January 23, 2019) | Rep. Alcee Hastings (D-FL) | Referred; no action |
| 115th-116th | Protecting Our Students in Schools Act (POSSA), first c. 2018 | Rep. A. Donald McEachin (D-VA), Rep. Suzanne Bonamici (D-OR), Sen. Chris Murphy (D-CT) | No action |
| 116th | H.R. 8460 (Sept 30, 2020) and S. 4936 (Dec 1, 2020) | McEachin, Bonamici; Murphy | Died in committee |
| 117th | H.R. 3836 (30 cosponsors) and S. 2029 (June 2021) | McEachin, Bonamici; Murphy | No action |
| 118th | H.R. 3596 (49 cosponsors) and S. 1762 (May 2023), 60+ endorsing organizations | Bonamici, McBath, Moore, Wilson; Murphy | No action |
| 119th | H.R. 3265, POSSA of 2025 (May 8, 2025), 21 cosponsors, all Democrats | Bonamici, McBath, Moore | Referred to Education and Workforce; no hearing; **no Senate companion introduced** as of September 2026 despite the press release |

**What POSSA does:** bans corporal punishment in any school receiving federal funds; private right of action; Attorney General and OCR enforcement; state and district reporting; grants for PBIS, trauma-informed and restorative alternatives.

**Executive branch:** Secretary King's letter (November 22, 2016) and Secretary Cardona's letter (March 24, 2023, still posted as of September 2026) urged states to end the practice. No regulation, no rescission, no later policy statement was found.

**Why they stall (analysis, not reporting):** every version has been single-party; each was referred to the House education committee and never given a markup under either party's majority; sponsors changed by attrition (McCarthy retired 2015, McEachin died 2022) rather than by success; the spending-clause design invites the local-control objection heard in every state debate. No committee statement explaining the stall was found.
