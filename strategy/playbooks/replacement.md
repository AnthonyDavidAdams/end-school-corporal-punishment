# Playbook: making "what instead" the easy answer

The opposition's strongest floor argument is a story about a school that lost control. The only durable answer is a school that stopped paddling and got calmer, with a name, a principal willing to say so, and a free package any district can copy.

1. **Collect the witnesses.** Every district that stopped is in `data/districts/`. Ask the superintendent or a principal for a two-paragraph account and permission to quote it; add it to `case-studies/` and `training/modules/00-why-change.md`.
2. **Ship the training with every ask.** A board or committee that hears "here is the free plan, here is a district that used it" has nothing to fear from a yes.
3. **Tie it to money that already exists.** Federal Title IV-A (Student Support and Academic Enrichment) funds and most states' MTSS or PBIS frameworks already pay for what `training/` teaches. Cite the state's own program page in testimony.
4. **Offer help, not judgment.** The training is written for the schools that still paddle, in their conditions. Never present it as remediation.
5. **Measure and publish.** Referral counts before and after, from the district's own log (`training/modules/08-data.md`). The next district's board wants to see the neighbor's numbers.
