# Outline

The booklet's argument, in the booklet's order, one idea per spread. The words on each right-hand page are the booklet's own sentences, cut, not rewritten. The cartoon on each left-hand page is specified in `panels.md`.

1. **Why a minister is writing this.** The federal count fell from more than a hundred thousand a year to under twenty thousand; the conversation that mattered was in a fellowship hall; the Bible says what it says, and also how to judge what we do with it.
2. **Teaching them to observe all things.** Disciple and discipline share a root; the Great Commission is rendered as teaching; "train up a child" is the teacher's word.
3. **Provoke not your children to wrath.** Ephesians 6:4 has a prohibition in front of its command.
4. **The fruit test.** Hebrews 12:11: chastening is judged by what it yields afterward. The whole book rests on it.
5. **The rod passages, read honestly.** All five, listed at the back; they say a rod, they say beat; scholars differ; two reasons the author does not need to settle it.
6. **His son. Thy son.** First reason: every rod verse is addressed to a parent, none to a school; the board's question is a different one.
7. **By their fruits.** Second reason: scripture says judge by the yield; one tree proves nothing about an orchard; the 2016 meta-analysis.
8. **In schools specifically.** The 2022 school meta-analysis: more acting out, more withdrawal, lower performance.
9. **Grown up.** The 2019 matched study; Colossians 3:21 and Galatians 6:7 beside the findings.
10. **Prove all things.** Nothing on the other side; the AAP; testing a practice is obedience, not doubt.
11. **The teacher's work.** Teach, fit the consequence, practice; the Sunday school teacher as witness; fifty-four controlled studies; the Maryland trial.
12. **What the churches have said.** Presbyterian 2012 (334 to 306), Methodist 2004 and the Oklahoma bishop, ELCA 2025, the Catholic dioceses; the churches with no position.
13. **For the believer on a school board.** The one sentence; eleven hundred boards; the records to ask for; move the sentence; the close vote.
14. **A page to read aloud.** Whole, unchanged.
15. **Passages cited.** The last page.
