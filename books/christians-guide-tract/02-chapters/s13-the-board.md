That is the whole policy, one sentence, and more than eleven hundred school boards in the seventeen permitting states have already adopted it. They are your neighbors, in Alabama and Arkansas and Georgia and Texas and Tennessee and Missouri and Oklahoma.

Before the vote, ask the superintendent in writing for the counts by school, grade, race, sex and disability. In the 2021-22 federal count, boys were about half of enrollment and four fifths of the children struck; Black children were fifteen of every hundred enrolled and twenty-six of every hundred struck. Move the sentence. Set the effective date ninety days out so the training happens first. And if the vote is close, remember that the Presbyterian vote was close too, and the church is still standing.
