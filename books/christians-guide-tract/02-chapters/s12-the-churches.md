The Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) adopted a Resolution Against Corporal Punishment in 2012, by three hundred thirty-four votes to three hundred six, and called on every state to prohibit it in schools. The United Methodist Church's 2004 General Conference urged its members to adopt discipline methods that do not include corporal punishment, and in 2024 the church's bishop for Oklahoma wrote that his church does not condone it. The Evangelical Lutheran Church in America's 2025 message on child protection names physical discipline as prohibited in its worshiping communities. The Catholic dioceses I checked prohibit it in their own schools.

The Southern Baptist Convention, the Episcopal Church, the Disciples of Christ and the Church of the Brethren have no position. Silence is not endorsement. It means the question has not come to the floor.
