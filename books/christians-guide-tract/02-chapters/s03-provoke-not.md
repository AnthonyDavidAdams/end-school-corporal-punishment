```{=latex}
\spreadHead{Provoke not your children to wrath}{Ephesians 6:4}
```

Paul says it to fathers with a warning on both sides: provoke not your children to wrath, but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. Nurture is raising. Admonition is instruction and warning.

The verse has a prohibition in front of its command. Do not provoke the child to wrath. Then raise and teach. A method that produces an angry child has failed the first half of the verse before we get to the second.
