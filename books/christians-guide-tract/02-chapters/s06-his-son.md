First: every one of those verses is addressed to a parent about that parent's own child. Not one is addressed to a school, a magistrate, a government, or an employee of the state holding someone else's child.

The question before a school board is not whether the Bible permits a father to correct his son. It is whether a public employee may strike a child who is not his, with a wooden paddle, as a matter of district policy. Proverbs does not speak to that.
