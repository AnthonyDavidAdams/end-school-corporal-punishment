In 2022 a team did the same work for school corporal punishment alone. Across twenty-one studies and more than sixty-seven thousand children, school paddling went with more acting out, and with more of the anxiety and withdrawal that teachers call quiet and later call depression.

Across twenty studies and nearly a million children, it went with lower school performance. A child who settles down and learns is the thing paddling is associated with less of.
