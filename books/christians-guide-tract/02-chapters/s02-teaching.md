In English, discipline and disciple are the same root; the Latin behind both means a pupil, a learner. When the risen Christ sends the eleven out, the King James renders his command as teaching. Discipline, in the sense the Bible cares about, is what makes a learner.

Train up a child in the way he should go. Train: the teacher's word. The question for any method of discipline is whether the child learns.
