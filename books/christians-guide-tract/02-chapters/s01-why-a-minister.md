I was an ordained minister in another life. I came to school corporal punishment by accident, around 2011, when someone sent me the federal count of children paddled in public schools. In those years it was more than a hundred thousand children a year. The last count is under twenty thousand.

The conversation that mattered most was never the one about the research. It was the one in a church fellowship hall, with a superintendent who told me, gently, that the Bible says what it says. He was right that it says it. I think the Bible also tells us how to judge what we do with it.
